# E26 preregistration: an edge-addition tolerance in the sense of Taeb et al., Section 6 (2026-11-07, frozen before the first full run)

**Question.** Does a tolerance to added edges, built the way Taeb, Guo and Henckel (arXiv:2511.10625v3, Section 6, p. 23 to 24, Figure 7) read the robustness of an adjustment set off a ball of supergraphs around the analyst's graph, grade the released queries the way the breakdown radius does? If it does, the paper's sentence that the radius is a different quantity from a graph-distance tolerance has no number behind it. (Reviewer h8pC, W3.)

**What Taeb et al. define, and what is computed here.** Their Section 6 has one example, no algorithm and no experiment: the poset of causal ADMGs made of the specified graph and its supergraphs (extra directed or bidirected edges), restricted by a temporal order (the covariates precede the treatment), and an adjustment set is called robust to the largest d_L such that it stays valid on every graph within that distance. Unrestricted, one bidirected edge between x and y defeats every adjustment set, so the unrestricted tolerance is 1 on every query; any comparison must name its restriction. We name ours: directed edges only, added to one DAG of the analyst's class, keeping one fixed causal order. We do not claim that one added edge is one covering step of their ADMG poset; the result is called "an edge-addition tolerance in the sense of their Section 6", not their d_L. No code of theirs is released.

**Population.** Every released row of `per_query_records.csv` with `status = ok` (3,585 queries, 27 networks, all 14 arms), knowledge state G0 rebuilt from the released files and checked against the row's hashes, counts, committed verdict, set size and validity at the true DAG. Secondary population: the ten elicited arms and D_RAND (the E10 population). No synthetic family: the measure needs a DAG, and it is taken inside each released query's class.

**The DAG.** D0 is one consistent extension of G0, chosen deterministically (orient the smallest undirected edge in its sorted direction, Meek-close, repeat). The causal order is the topological order of D0 with ties broken by node name.

**Tolerance.** For the committed set Z of the query (the released set, valid in G0 and so in D0): the least number k of directed edges a -> b, absent from D0, each respecting the causal order, whose addition to D0 makes Z fail the generalized adjustment criterion for (x, y). Exhaustive iterative deepening to depth 3; exact because validity depends only on the subgraph induced by the ancestors of {x, y} and Z, so a minimal breaking set can be generated one edge at a time with each head among the current ancestors. Budget 120 s per query; a search that ends without a break at depth 3 gives the category "4 or more"; a search stopped by time gives a lower bound and the query is "undecided". Secondary move set: any directed edge that keeps the graph acyclic (computed where the order tolerance is above 1; it is 1 wherever the order tolerance is 1), 60 s. Extension sensitivity: the depth-1 order verdict on two seeded random extensions of G0, 30 s each.

**Radius.** The released `r_hop` under the GAC (unreached = infinite, the largest value); rows with `hop_exact = 0` (censored radius) are left out of the rank statistics and counted.

**Registered statistic.** On queries with an exact radius and a decided tolerance (tolerance above 3 coded 4): Kendall tau_b between radius and order tolerance, with a network-clustered bootstrap 95 % interval (2,000 resamples, seed 20261107). Beside it, the share of query pairs ordered strictly by the radius that the tolerance does not order the same way (tied or reversed), the per-query share where the tolerance is below, equal to or above the radius (radius capped at 4), and the share of queries with tolerance 1 with its Clopper-Pearson interval (and its worst case counting undecided queries as not 1). Also reported: the cross-tabulation, the acyclic tolerance, the extension sensitivity, the share by arm, and, as a secondary on these released rows only, the AUC of 1/r and of 1/t for invalidity at the true DAG (the card's AUC on the learned-class rows of E5 is not part of E26).

**Prediction.** The tolerance is 1 on at least 80 % of decided queries, and tau_b is at most 0.3. Reason: in a DAG with a fixed order, one added edge already defeats Z whenever the treatment precedes a member of Z that is an ancestor of the outcome (the edge x -> z makes z a causal node, so Z contains a forbidden node), or whenever some node before x that is not in Z is connected to x by an open path (one edge into y opens a non-causal path); committed sets are built from parents of causal nodes, so one of the two holds on most queries. The radius instead counts knowledge retractions, which is 1 on about 56 % of released rows and 2 or more or infinite on the rest; a near-constant tolerance cannot reproduce that ordering.

**Against the authors.** If tau_b is at least 0.8 with a bootstrap lower limit above 0.7, about 90 % of pairs are ordered alike and the novelty sentence against Taeb et al. must say that the radius is a cheaper route to the same ranking, not a different quantity. That result is reported to the authors as a result against the novelty claim and analysed; it is not printed. If the share at tolerance 1 is below 50 %, the expected degeneracy is wrong and the comparison is reported in full instead of as "it does not grade".

**Seeds and exclusions.** D_RAND knowledge seed 20260913 (released). Random extensions seeded by a hash of the query key (salt 20261107). Rows whose rebuild does not match the release are reported, not analysed, and make the run exit non-zero.

**Stopping rule.** One pass over all queries; a resume only reruns units that raised. No budget change after the first full run; any later rerun is reported beside this one in DEVIATIONS.md.

**Consistency failures (exit 1).** A rebuild mismatch; Z not valid in D0 under the submitted or the released GAC oracle; a breaking edge set that either reference oracle (submitted `is_gac_valid_dag`, released networkx `is_valid_adjustment_set_gac_dag`) does not confirm as breaking; an order witness that violates the order; a sampled non-breaking single edge (first ten per query) that the released oracle calls breaking.
