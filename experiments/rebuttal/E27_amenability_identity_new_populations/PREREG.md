# E27 preregistration: the amenability identity on populations the first author did not cover (2026-11-07, frozen before the first full run)

**Question.** Proposition C.7.1(iv) says the nearest state where the committed set fails admits no valid adjustment set; with Perković et al. (2017, 2018) a valid set exists in an MPDAG exactly when (X, Y) is amenable and the canonical set is valid. The first author found the radius of the committed set (r_val) equal to the amenability radius (r_amen, the fewest retractions of the analyst's orientations after which (X, Y) is no longer amenable, a quantity that does not involve Z) on all 6,949 of his queries (release Table 1 queries and the synthetic instances; `results/rebuttal/e10/amenability_radius.csv` of his branch). Does the identity hold on queries he did not score, and is r_amen cheaper to compute? Reviewer points h8pC-W3, h8pC-SUM, qQYf-Q2.

**Population A (registered as asked by the plan): networks outside the paper's 33.** Every network file the repository can load (the 40 files pinned in `results/axisa3/networks/acquisition_manifest.json`, pgmpy 1.0.0 sdist, sha256 checked; no other downloader exists in the repository and no new network is downloaded) whose name is not among the 33 networks of the frozen CPDAG-only frame `results/frame/frame.jsonl` (sha256 6f05f044…43fc). Queries are chosen by the paper's CPDAG-only rule, reimplemented unchanged from `experiments/frame_build.py`: treatments in or next to a chain component of size at least 2, outcomes the possible descendants of the treatment, cap 20 by seeded stride. Disclosure: a structural probe run before this file was written (parse, CPDAG, component sizes only; no radius) found 7 such files: M-bias (bidirected, not a DAG), and cancer, confounding, earthquake, mildew, pigs and survey, whose CPDAGs have no undirected edge. Population A is therefore expected to be empty, and the run records that census rather than assuming it.

**Population B (the card's new population, where the identity is actually tested).** The frozen CPDAG-only frame (648 pairs, 33 networks) crossed with all ten elicited knowledge states of `results/elicit/knowledge.json` (sha256 45119393…bfc7bb), states built exactly as E11 builds them (`llm_survival.build_state`, G0 = Meek(CPDAG, K), committed set Z* = optimal set of G0 with valid empty sets committed, `real_survival.commit_z_star`). The first author scored only Table 1 queries; the subset of B outside the Table 1 frame (pairs not in `results/axis_robustness_real/frame.jsonl`) is reported separately as the strictly new part.

**Computed per unit.** For every unit with a G0: r_amen by the submitted upward search (`radius_local_up_fast`, failure = `is_amenable` false, UNREACHED when (X, Y) is amenable at the CPDAG itself), as in the first author's `e10b_amenability.py`. For every unit with a committed Z*: r_val by the submitted `breakdown_radius`, criterion gac, 300 s limit, as in E11. Each call under a 600 s wall clock and a per-worker memory cap; wall time of each call recorded. Independent check: when G0 commits at most 12 orientations, both radii are also computed by plain enumeration of the closed subsets of K_G0 (no shared search code).

**Registered statistic.** On population B: the share of units with a committed Z* and both radii exact (UNREACHED counts as a value) where r_val = r_amen, with a 95% Clopper-Pearson interval; the count of disagreements. Beside it: the same share on the finite-radius units, on the units outside the Table 1 frame, and per condition; the distribution of r_val (counts at 1, 2, 3, ..., UNREACHED) and of r_amen on all units with a G0; the median and 90th percentile wall time per query of each computation and the median paired ratio t_val / t_amen. On population A: the number of networks, of candidate pairs and of scored units.

**Prediction.** Share = 1.000 with zero disagreements (the identity follows from C.7.1(iv) plus Perković et al. only if the canonical-set branch never binds; it never did in 6,949 queries). Enumeration agrees with both searches on every unit where it runs. Population A has zero queries. Timing: the card's bar is a median paired speed-up of at least 2; we expect it to be met, but we register it as uncertain because E11's median r_val time was already 2 ms.

**Against the authors.** One disagreement where both radii are exact: the corollary "the radius is the distance to loss of amenability" is not printed; the unit is reported with its witnesses, and the two-branch form (not amenable, or canonical set invalid) is the only statement the reply may make. A disagreement between enumeration and either search is a code defect, reported before any number is used. A median speed-up below 2: the reply does not claim a computational gain.

**Exclusions.** None by result. Units without G0 (no knowledge, contradictory knowledge, intractable extensions, network absent from the bundle) and units without a committed Z* are written with their status and counted. Censored calls (wall clock, memory) are counted and excluded from the share, and the share with every censored unit counted as a disagreement is printed beside it.

**Stopping rule.** One pass. A resume reruns only units not on disk. No budget change after the first full run; any later pass is reported beside this one in DEVIATIONS.md.
