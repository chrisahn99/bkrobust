# E25 deviations from PREREG.md

1. **2026-11-07, about 02:05 CET, after 1,466 of 16,502 rows of the first full run.** The level scan exhausted the whole up-set of G0 on queries whose radius is infinite, so a single 107-node query took 131 s (2,054 states) and the run stalled on larger ones. Change: when the committed set is valid and (x, y) amenable at the top state (the CPDAG), the scan is capped at 2,000 states and 20 s; if the cap is reached, both radii are recorded as infinite by the submitted top-state rule and the row is marked `decided_by_top_state`, which `analyse.py` counts separately. A state found failing or non-amenable under such a top state is a consistency failure. Queries with a finite radius keep the registered budget (200,000 states, 300 s). The 1,466 rows already written were computed under the stricter rule and are kept; the run was resumed, not restarted. The registered statistics (a) and (b) are unchanged; (a) is vacuous on infinite-radius queries, and (b) on them now partly rests on the top-state rule, so (b) on finite radii (`b_finite_only`) is the number to quote.
2. **Known, not a change.** Nine released rows (for instance Kampen_2014, ALN to PER) have a GAC radius that differs from the released `r_hop`; E10 records the same nine as consistency failures. They make `run.py` exit 1 under the registered consistency rule and are listed in `results.json`; they do not bear on (a) or (b), which compare two quantities computed on the same state.
