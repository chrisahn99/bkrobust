# E25 preregistration: the nearest failing states are the non-amenable states (2026-11-07, frozen before the first full run)

**Question.** Proposition C.7.1(iv) says that every failing state above G0 admits no valid adjustment set, so the radius is the distance from G0 to the nearest state where no set identifies the effect by adjustment. Perković, Kalisch and Maathuis (UAI 2017) make amenability necessary for adjustment in a maximal PDAG (Definition 4.3, b-amenability; Theorem 4.4, soundness and completeness). Perković (UAI 2020, arXiv:1910.02997v3) proves for single X and Y that when Y is not a parent of X, an adjustment set exists if and only if the graph is amenable, and then Pa(X, G) is one (Proposition 4.2, with Proposition 3.2 and Theorem 3.6). Together they predict that for every query the nearest failing states of the committed set are exactly the non-amenable states at that distance, and that the radius equals the amenability radius. Reviewer points h8pC-W3, h8pC-SUM, qQYf-Q2.

**Populations.**
1. Released: every row of `_data/per_query_records.csv` with status ok in the ten elicited arms and D_RAND (the E10 frame, 3,014 rows), state rebuilt from the released files and checked against the row's hashes; committed set from the released rule (`committed_adjustment_set`).
2. E11 frames: every (condition, network, x, y) of the CPDAG-only frame `results/frame/frame.jsonl` and of the Table 1 frame `results/axis_robustness_real/frame.jsonl`, under the eight real-name elicited conditions, state and committed set Z* exactly as E11 (`ls.build_state`, `rs.commit_z_star`); a pair present in both frames is scored once. Units without a committed set are written with their status and not analysed.
3. Synthetic: the 4,584 stored survival instances (4,104 targeted, `results/axis_robustness_p6/survival_instances.csv`; 480 untargeted, `results/axis_robustness_unconfounded_v2/survival_instances.csv`), rebuilt from seed as E15, Z = the stored Z*.
The first author's 6,949 queries (`_data/chris_e10/amenability_radius.csv`, sha256 55b19b1e…c4dd7e, his commit d1ef6e4) are his 2,365 release rows (contained in population 1, joined on network, arm, x, y) and the 4,584 synthetic instances (population 3, joined on instance id).

**Measurement.** For each query, the up-set of G0 is enumerated level by level with the submitted upper-cover generator (`local_up_covers_fast`): level j holds the states reached by j covers, and every state is checked to have exactly j orientations of K_G0 retracted. On every state of every level up to the one where both events have occurred, we evaluate whether Z fails (submitted GAC predicate) and whether (x, y) is not amenable (submitted `is_amenable`). r_val is the first level holding a failing state and r_amen the first holding a non-amenable state; infinite (UNREACHED) when the up-set is exhausted first. Budget per query: 200,000 states and 300 s; a level cut by the budget is incomplete. Beside the scan, and timed separately per query: one amenability call at G0; the submitted radius (`breakdown_radius`, criterion gac, as E10: ladder only up to 60 nodes, else top-state check plus a budgeted upward search of 20,000 closures and 120 s); and the amenability radius by the first author's rule (top-state check, then the same upward search with failure = not amenable).

**Registered statistic.** Over all analysed queries, and per population:
(a) the share of queries with a finite r_val whose r_val level is complete in which every failing state at that level is non-amenable;
(b) the share of queries with both radii decided (from the scan, else from the exact search results) where r_val = r_amen;
and, on the first author's 6,949 queries, (b') the share where our r_val equals his per-query amenability radius `r_amen`. 95% Clopper-Pearson intervals.

**Prediction.** 1.000 on (a), (b) and (b'). This is a consequence of Proposition C.7.1(iv) with Perković (2020, Prop. 4.2): at a state G above G0, Pa(x, G) is contained in Pa(x, G0), y is not in Pa(x, G0) because a set is valid at G0, so if G were amenable Pa(x, G) would be valid there, and no set is.

**Against the authors.** Any exception is listed with its CPDAG, G0, x, y, Z and the amenable failing state, analysed, and reported to the authors. One exception ends the identity: then the appendix keeps "by adjustment" without the amenability sentence, and the answer to h8pC-W3 says the radius and the amenability radius coincide on the stated share, not always.

**Secondary (reported, not registered).** Shares decided and undecided; the GAC reason at each nearest failing state; agreement between the submitted `is_amenable` and a literal implementation of Definitions 3.1 and 4.3 of Perković et al. (2017) (b-possibly causal paths, exponential, step budget 200,000) at every nearest failing state; at every amenable state visited, whether Pa(x, G) is GAC-valid when y is not a parent of x (Prop. 4.2 checked in code); our r_amen and r_val against his columns; median and total seconds of the one-call test, the radius and the amenability search, and the median paired ratios.

**Consistency failures (exit 1).** A level state whose retracted count differs from its level; Z failing or (x, y) non-amenable at G0; the scan's radius differing from an exact submitted radius or from an exact amenability search; a released row whose rebuild or exact radius differs from the release; a synthetic instance whose x or Z* differs from the stored one.

**Stopping rule.** One pass. A resume reruns only units that raised. No budget changes after the first full row; any later change goes in DEVIATIONS.md with its date.
