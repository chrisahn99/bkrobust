# E29 preregistration: the radius against the undirected degree of X where separation is undefined (2026-11-07, frozen before the first full run)

Serves reviewer C2Bd, weakness 3, and h8pC, weakness 3 (a comparison with a trivial graph statistic is the novelty question asked locally).

**Question.** On the units where the separation proxy is undefined, E16 measured a weak pooled ranking of survival by the radius (tau_b +0.134 [-0.033, +0.419], 1,033 units, 14 networks) and found the radius equal to 1 on 821 of them. Is the radius on these units more than a count a reader can take off the CPDAG by inspection, namely the number of undirected edges at X? And does the nearest failure go through X's own orientations?

**Population.** E16's undefined population, selected by E16's rule on the same committed files: real-name units of the submitted Table 1 cohort (`results/axis_robustness_llm/analysis_units.csv`, sha256 58de266a…7658) with a radius, a survival AUC (`AUC_frac`) and no separation value, the radius being the exact GAC radius of `results/radius_gac_recompute/` (checked equal to the stored radius). Expected n = 1,033; any other count stops the run before any statistic.

**Computed per unit.** The state is rebuilt as the GAC recompute rebuilt it (`real_survival.load_network`, `llm_survival.build_state` on `results/elicit/knowledge.json`, sha256 45119393…bfc7bb); the committed set Z is read from the GAC recompute file and checked against `real_survival.commit_z_star`. Recorded: the undirected degree of X in the CPDAG (deg_u, the number of undirected edges at X); the size of X's chain component; the number of Z members in that component (recorded, not required to be 0: the undefined flag is the table's `separation` column, computed with the set committed when the panel ran, while Z here is the GAC recompute's set; the smoke found 2 of 28 units, Polzer_2012 with X = Psychosocial, where the GAC set holds one member of X's component. Such units stay in the population, as in E16, are counted, and the registered statistics are repeated without them as a sensitivity line); the analyst's orientations at X, K_X (edges undirected in the CPDAG and directed in G0 that touch X); the GAC radius by an upward search that returns its witness state, in the order of the submitted `radius_local_up_fast`, against which radius and witness are checked; every failing upper cover of G0 (the distance-1 witnesses) with the orientations it retracts; the failure mechanism of each witness: amenability lost (`is_amenable` false), else a member of Z in the GAC forbidden set, else a non-causal path left open.

**Registered statistics.**
1. Kendall tau_b between the radius and deg_u on the pooled units, 95% percentile interval from a network-cluster bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, seed 0, computed by the submitted `real_analyse.tau_for_stratum` (the Table 1 code).
2. Among radius-1 units, the share whose analyst orientations at X are exactly one orientation and some distance-1 witness retracts it ("the only orientation at X is the one retracted"), with a Clopper-Pearson interval and a network-cluster bootstrap interval.

**Secondary statistics.** (a) The tie ceiling: the largest tau_b(radius, AUC) attainable with the observed marginals (sorted values paired), and the observed tau_b as a share of it. (b) Witness mechanism counts by radius, and over all distance-1 witnesses. (c) The planned falsifier: on E16's within-state population (states (network, condition) where the radius varies over all scorable units, restricted to the undefined units, 2 or more kept; E16 found 757 units in 48 states, and that point, +0.083, must be reproduced), the within-state tau_b(radius, AUC) minus tau_b(deg_u, AUC), same network draws, 10,000 resamples, seed 0. The plan counted 34 such states; E16's definition, which gives 48, is used and the difference is noted here, not chosen after the run. (d) Pooled tau_b of deg_u with AUC, of component size with radius and with AUC, of |K_X| with radius; cross-tabulations of radius by deg_u and by |K_X|.

**Prediction (written before any E29 number exists).** (1) tau_b(radius, deg_u) positive, between +0.05 and +0.35: a vertex with more undirected edges gives Meek's rules more ways to re-imply a retracted orientation, so one retraction suffices less often; lower limit above 0 more likely than not, held with low confidence. (2) The share is at least 0.5: most radius-1 units have one analyst orientation at X and losing it breaks the set. (b) At least 70% of distance-1 witnesses lose amenability (consistent with E25/E27, where the radius equalled the amenability radius). (c) The paired within-state difference is positive at its point; whether its lower limit clears 0 is uncertain (probability about 0.4).

**Outcomes against the method.** If (c)'s lower limit is at or below 0, the reply makes no claim that the radius ranks survival where separation is undefined beyond what deg_u does; it keeps only "the radius returns a value where separation does not", and the result is analysed, not printed. If tau_b(radius, deg_u) has a lower limit above +0.5, the reply says the radius on these units is mostly a local count at X. If the share in (2) is below 0.5, the planned sentence "the nearest failure goes through X's own edges" is not used.

**Exclusions.** None by result. Units whose state cannot be rebuilt or whose call hits the 600 s wall clock or the memory cap are written with their status and counted; every consistency failure (Z differs from `commit_z_star`, radius differs from the stored one or from `radius_local_up_fast`, witness differs, radius 1 without a distance-1 witness or the reverse, |K_G0| differs from the table) is reported before any number is used.

**Stopping rule.** One pass. A resume reruns only units not yet written with status ok. No change of statistic, population or seed after the first full run; any later pass is reported beside this one in DEVIATIONS.md.

**Disclosure before freezing.** The smoke (4 of 94 (condition, network) items, 28 units, 4 networks) was run and read before this file was hashed, to check the code: it showed tau_b(radius, deg_u) +0.76 on those 28 units and the share in (2) at 17 of 19. The prediction above was written before the smoke and is left as written. The only change made after the smoke is the Z-in-component item above, from a consistency failure to a recorded count.
