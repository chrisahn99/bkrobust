## open questions
- Does a density- and size-matched ER control still separate from real structure? At the sparse setting ER already reaches 24.1% against real 45.4%, under a factor of two rather than the 'two and a half to three times' the report claims. If the matched answer is 24.1 vs 45.4 the finding narrows from 'ER is not exchangeable with real structure' to 'the density parameter was set wrong', which is a tuning defect and not a substrate argument.
- Does Decision 2 survive the audit? It was taken on the strength of the unmatched comparison. If the matched comparison narrows the gap, structure-matched synthetic generators may need re-opening rather than staying declined.
- Which structural statistics must a substrate match for a claim about r to transfer? Separation from treatment to the nearest member of O* inside the chain component is the leading candidate, because Henckel's O* = pa(cn)\forb is local — but no published criterion exists for graph-family transfer, only for effect transportability.
- Is 45.4% a property of real causal structure or a property of curated benchmark networks? Undecidable from one sdist. Needs a second, independently acquired corpus (bnRep, OCDB, CausalBench) before the finding is a population claim.
- What are the var- and R^2-sortability profiles of the corpus under bnlearn's own fitted coefficients? The pilot controlled var-sortability on synthetic data and neither statistic has ever been computed on the real corpus.
- Should M-bias be recovered rather than dropped? It is the one network in the corpus whose structure is the framework's motivating failure, and it is excluded by an is_dag parse flag with no written argument.
- Does the minority-applicability fact (median 10.7% undirected, 6 of 39 fully compelled) scope the framework honestly, or does it need a stated criterion for when a network is worth running at all?
- MAGIC-NIAB and ANDES are both in our corpus and are the parent paper's realistic networks — should that continuity be claimed explicitly, and does it constrain which networks may be called novel?
- Do the 4 continuous bnjson networks carry enough structural diversity to support any statement about r_0, given that the one network with a large under-determined component (arth150) censored?
- Should the ER arm survive at all as a negative control, given Decision 2 declined structure-matched synthetic generators? Keeping it costs a labelled stratum and buys the positive-control power check that Test A6-3 depends on.

# A3 — KNOWLEDGE MODEL — where K comes from: source, coverage, granularity, and above all its error process
## constraints
- [ARGUED] CHECKABLE #1, admissibility. Consistency is a purely graphical, syntactic predicate and says nothing about truth. Verbatim: 'Background knowledge R is consistent with maximal PDAG G if and only if Algorithm 1 does not return a FAIL (Meek, 1995).' The knowledge model must be able to emit K that passes this and is false, and its error must be reported as the fraction of CONSISTENT-BUT-FALSE assertions, never as 'noise'.
  SUPPORT: Perkovic, Kalisch & Maathuis UAI 2017, arXiv:1707.02171, full text read; wiki/literature/causality/perkovic-2017-interpreting-cpdags-background-knowledge.md

## open questions
- Which unit: d_L (model inclusion), d_L,pseudo (orient/un-orient atomic moves), or a fixed-skeleton sub-poset distance? They provably differ, and the 48-element demo only coincidentally collapses them.
- Is 'breakdown radius' a name collision? - What is the perturbation operator on a TIER (a block retraction) and on an ancestral constraint? Both are named granularities and neither has a defined move.
- Is there a soft-constraint geometry — a penalty ball — matching our own measured finding that soft beats hard under noisy knowledge, and matching RoaDS's adjacency-matrix penalisation?
- What sentinel semantics do r_opt, r_eps and r_0 each need, declared in conventions.py BEFORE they are measured rather than after?
- Should the two geometries be unified, and if so which one is the definition and which the approximation?
- How is the 0.43-0.47 conservativeness reported — as part of the certificate, or as an acknowledged slack? A guarantee that is right 100% of the time and tight 56% of the time is two numbers, not one.

## open questions
- Does arXiv:2503.09453's set of seven datasets with expert-validated causal graphical structures carry a reference independent of the expert? If yes, A6 changes from BUILD to CALIBRATE and the whole headline of this axis narrows. This was the gap-fill pass's own top-ranked follow-up and it was left unread.
- What external referent judges the simulated KNOWLEDGE distribution? The sortability paper's 'what pattern should real systems show' is the only template in the corpus; the analogue question — what error pattern should real analysts show — has no answer anywhere.
- Does the certificate change a decision? Without a decision-relevant quantity in the audit arm, the framing repeats the exact failure mode that sank our own restrained paper. EVPPI is the off-the-shelf answer and needs a loss function the project has not written.
- Which of the 39 networks can a frontier model reproduce verbatim from its name alone? The recall probe has never been run, so the fame stratification Test A6-4 depends on has no data behind it.
- Is the surrogate's error rate different on the applied dagitty DAGs than on the BIF benchmarks, and if so is the difference fame, domain, or size (dagitty files are 4-18 nodes, the BIF corpus runs to 1,041)?
- What tolerance counts as 'unlike'? A permutation test on enough instances always rejects, so the tolerance on the radius summary must be pre-registered under the repo's two-stage commitment discipline.
- Is the harvested-publication sample large enough to test at all? Eleven or twelve networks, one of which yields a two-variable adjustment set, is a very thin second sample and the power of any two-sample test on it is unknown.
- Does Su & Henckel's chi-square baseline run on MPDAGs? It is stated for linear SEMs on DAGs and the transfer is unverified; without it the necessity arm cannot be scored.
- Should the referent be the analyst's K BEFORE or AFTER the Meek consistency filter? Both are analyst-observable, only the post-filter object is what a practitioner ends up using, and up to two thirds of corrupted draws are rejected outright by that filter.
- Is a confidence-calibration curve estimable from one elicitation pass per network, or does it require repeated elicitation — and does repeated elicitation still count as one K?
- How is calibration reported conditional on r rather than marginally, given that the small-radius corner is where a uniform interval degenerates?
- Can a contamination screen be run over the 39 networks, given that the available method needs at least two models?
- Does the audit need an interventional or effect-level leg (physical causal chambers, single-cell perturbation benchmarks), or is that beyond the framework's scope? The field is moving that way and the answer should be deliberate.
- Should an ER negative-control arm survive into the redesign, and in what form? A claim that separates real from a MATCHED ER is stronger evidence than one that holds only against an unmatched ER, and the current arm cannot support either reading.
