# Beat the BFS First

## THESIS
This protocol measures whether a certificate denominated in the analyst's own retracted claims, computed on a candidate frame and a knowledge set that contain no true-DAG quantity, beats the free statistics a practitioner already has, and whether its promise survives the truth being revealed. That is the right thing to measure because the framework's entire value proposition is a per-query number an analyst can act on, and on data already committed to the tree a fifteen-line breadth-first search on the estimated CPDAG reproduces that number exactly on 636 of 831 instances and to within one on 97.8% — so until the radius is shown to beat that on a paired, network-clustered, audited outcome, every other axis is an expensive way of re-deriving a hop count.

# A1 — SUBSTRATE — the population of true structures and parameters, and the statistics a substrate must match for a claim about r to transfer

## DECISION
Four labelled strata never pooled into a headline, one labelled instrument arm outside the substrate, and three reporting rules that bind every number the protocol prints. T0 ILLUSTRATION: the four dagitty pedagogical files (confounding, mediator, paths, and M-bias recovered as an explicit five-node latent expansion with provenance recorded as ours). T1 BENCHMARK: the 24 BIF networks, labelled a corpus claim because all of them trace to one pgmpy 1.0.0 sdist. T2 APPLIED: the eight author-named dagitty DAGs (Acid_1996, Didelez_2010, Kampen_2014, Polzer_2012, Schipf_2010, Sebastiani_2005, Shrier_2008, Thoemmes_2013). T3 REAL-PARAMETER: the four bnjson networks whose coefficients are fits to real measurements (ecoli70, arth150, magic-niab, magic-irri) plus Sachs as an evaluation referent with precision and recall against its reference network refused in writing. N-ER-MATCHED is retained but is NOT a substrate tier: it is instrument calibration for the A6 power check, matched on EXPECTED DEGREE across the real corpus's measured band (mean degree 1.60 to 9.86, median 2.92, verified by recomputation over the 39 DAG rows of descriptive_structure.csv) and on size cells reaching at least 80% of admissible instance mass, behind a committed generator script, never in the denominator of a substrate claim. That single sentence is the reconciliation Decision 2 needs and it is written once. THREE BINDING REPORTING RULES. (i) COMMON DENOMINATOR: every separation statistic is reported over ALL admissible instances with the undefined stratum in the denominator, never conditional on measurable. Verified: separation >= 2 is 45.4% of the 463 measurable instances but 25.3% of all 831 (210/831), and the undefined rate differs 2.6-fold between the arms (44.3% real, 368 of 831, against 17.1% random-generator), so a conditional comparison conditions each side on a differently selected subset. (ii) THE NETWORK IS THE UNIT OF EVIDENCE: measured one-way-ANOVA intraclass correlation is 0.3978 on the r=1 indicator (k=25, N=831, m0=30.51, MSB=3.179, MSW=0.150) and 0.5443 on separation >= 2, giving design effects 13.8 and 17.1 and effective sample sizes 60.1 and 48.7; every rate carries a network cluster bootstrap (B=4,000) and prints its design effect, per-network numbers are printed as COUNTS and never as percentages (the median yielding network holds 11 instances, SE 0.147), and the ceiling n_eff -> k/rho = 62.8 is printed once so the reader knows further pair sampling buys nothing. (iii) DISPERSION KIND IS NAMED IN EVERY CELL: seed dispersion exists only where a seed exists, between-network dispersion is all a fixed-corpus cell has, and a deterministic radius on a fixed C-hat and a fixed K has neither, so no plus-or-minus is printed beside it. A descriptive table is committed before the first radius, carrying per network the mean degree, undirected fraction, chain-component size distribution, separation composition as three levels (undefined / 1 / >= 2), and var- and R-squared-sortability wherever continuous parameters exist, with sortability printed as UNDEFINED on the 24 discrete BIF networks rather than omitted. Every substrate statistic computed from the true DAG carries manifest arm = substrate_design, a third value the binary {deployment, audit} field cannot express.

## WHY
The tier split is not stylistic; it is forced by a measurement I recomputed from results/axisa3/instances.jsonl. All 16 admissible instances at r >= 5 lie in `paths`, a 17-node dagitty textbook drawing whose CPDAG is 73.7% undirected with a single 15-node chain component and which supplies 20 of the 831 instances. Remove the three illustrations and the maximum radius over the remaining 807 instances is 4, with 94.4% at r <= 2 and zero instances at r >= 5. The sentence in circulation — real structure reaching radius 14 against a synthetic maximum — therefore compares two designed spines, and on the ledger's own corrected synthetic figures (random-generator maximum 5, pooled maximum 13 supplied entirely by the designed backdoor family) the real corpus minus one drawing has a SMALLER maximum than the random generators. The common-denominator rule is forced by the ledger's own 2.6-fold undefined-rate finding. The clustering correction is forced by the measured concentration of the corpus: the top five networks supply 552 of 831 instances (barley 189, insurance 160, diabetes 89, Polzer_2012 66, magic-niab 48), so a binomial interval on 831 is a fiction. Sortability is mandatory per Reisach et al. NeurIPS 2021 (arXiv:2102.13647, var-sortability 0.57 real against above 0.94 synthetic) and NeurIPS 2023 (arXiv:2303.18211), with iSCM's reversed R-squared-sortability on denser graphs (Ormaniec arXiv:2406.11601, Herman CLeaR 2025 arXiv:2503.17037) requiring a second parameterisation; Myriam Tami is a MICS colleague and a plausible reviewer, so an unreported sortability profile is the cheapest way to lose the substrate section. Chen et al. ICLR 2022 (arXiv:2110.03239) supplies the transfer sentence that replaces 'ER is unrealistic': zero-sample transfer succeeds if and only if the target lies in the SUPPORT of the randomisation, so tiers are chosen to COVER the deployed target rather than to be broad. M-bias is recovered because it is the textbook structure in which a consistent-looking adjustment choice opens a collider path, which is the exact failure being priced, and because its exclusion is currently an is_dag parse-flag default with no written argument.

## REJECTED_ALTERNATIVES
Pooling T0 into the headline: rejected because it moves the maximum radius from 4 to 14 and the r >= 5 count from 0 to 16, the same failure mode as the verified precedent where pooling the designed backdoor family with five random generators moved an r=1 headline from 79.6% to 65.3%. Reporting separation conditional on the measurable subset: rejected because it nearly doubles the apparent effect and conditions each arm on a differently selected population. Keeping the existing unmatched Erdos-Renyi comparator: rejected because it is unmatched on size, coverage, search method, gate provenance and pipeline, and because no committed script in the repository writes results/axisa2/random_control.jsonl, so its K-selection rule is unverifiable from the tree — the one place this project's audit apparatus fails the third-party-rerun standard it imposes on everyone else. Promoting the matched ER arm to a substrate tier: rejected because Decision 2 declined structure-matched synthetic generators, and the instrument-calibration label is the only form in which it survives. A per-instance denominator for intervals: rejected on the measured design effect of 13.8. Bare point fractions: rejected per Sadeghi and AbdAlmageed (arXiv:2603.17405), whose reported 95.1 +/- 2.4 reproduced at 69.1 +/- 21.7.

## FALSIFIABLE_CHECK
THE MATCHED-CONTROL DIFFERENCE TEST, stated on the DIFFERENCE with clustering on both sides and over the full denominator, pre-registered before the control is looked at. DECISION RULE: if the cluster-bootstrap 95% interval on (real minus matched-ER) separation >= 2, both computed over full denominators, contains zero, the non-exchangeability premise is WITHDRAWN in writing, the finding is restated as 'the edge probability was set wrong' rather than 'Erdos-Renyi is not exchangeable with real structure', the topology point is cited to Stuempel et al. (arXiv:2501.02019) rather than claimed, Decision 2 is formally reopened for Ze rather than silently retained, and A6's positive control loses its power so every A6-1 non-rejection is reported as uninterpretable at the same moment. This rule is designed to bite: the real side is 25.3% with a network cluster-bootstrap interval of [7.8%, 46.1%], so at k=25 no difference under roughly 20 points is resolvable, and the sparse ER cell is already at 24.1% conditional. SECOND RULE, the illustration gate: report the maximum radius and the r >= 3 count twice, with and without T0, on every table. If no network outside T0 produces an instance at r >= 5 under real elicited knowledge — already true in the committed corpus, where 45 of the 63 r >= 3 instances sit outside T0 and none reaches 5 — the paper states plainly that the certificate's measured range on real structure is {1,2,3,4}, and every sentence about large radii is a sentence about a textbook drawing. THIRD RULE, sortability: any arm carrying continuous parameters showing var-sortability above 0.94 has its parameterisation replaced (iSCM with R-squared-sortability reported per density cell, or UUMC) before a single result is reported. FOURTH RULE, the population embargo: no real-versus-synthetic population sentence is written until the number of instance-yielding networks reaches 52, the count at which a 10-point gap becomes resolvable at the measured ICC (n_eff = 96); below that the axis reports the real side descriptively and makes no comparison. FIFTH RULE, M-bias: if the latent expansion yields no admissible instance in which a consistent K makes O(G0) open a collider path, the recovery bought nothing and is recorded as a negative result in that sentence.

## COST
Compute is negligible and measured: CPDAG construction over all 39 parsing networks is 60.95 s total, slowest single network 0.152 s. The matched ER grid is roughly 24 cells times a pilot-derived seed count, hours on one machine, and it is smaller than the 2,880-draw arm it replaces. Cluster bootstrap at B=4,000 over 25 to 52 clusters is seconds. Sortability probes are minutes per continuous network. Agent-hours about 14: the committed matched-generator script, the M-bias latent-expansion fixture with its written causal-sufficiency argument, the sortability probes, the cluster-bootstrap and design-effect reporting layer, and the canonical numbers file. Human-hours about 6: the M-bias and selection-node scope arguments, the acquisition licence review, and the edge-for-edge check of sachs.bif.gz against the Sachs consensus network (the BIF network is discrete and the 853 observations are continuous, so they may describe different graphs). CUT AND RECORDED: no elicitation is run on any acquired second corpus. That confines its benefit to the substrate claim and leaves the audit headline at 25 yielding networks, which is stated in the budget rather than discovered by a referee.

## OPEN
Whether a second, independently acquired corpus reaches 52 instance-yielding networks at the observed yield (25 of 39 today), which is the only route from a corpus claim to a population claim. Whether M-bias's latent expansion and the single `selected` annotation in Didelez_2010 — verified as the only selection marker in the corpus — are recovered or excluded in writing; they break causal sufficiency in opposite directions and the honest outcome may be two written exclusions rather than two recoveries. Whether sachs.bif.gz matches the Sachs consensus network edge for edge, which decides whether the T3 anchor is one object or two.

# A2 — QUERY POLICY — how the treatment-outcome pair is drawn, non-adaptively, and what the admissibility filters do to the population

## DECISION
ONE candidate frame drawn from an ESTIMATED CPDAG and nothing else, frozen before any knowledge is elicited, with every knowledge-dependent condition demoted from a filter to a printed per-instance STATUS on that one frozen list. (1) The reference graph is C-hat_E: one designated discovery run per network at the largest Pi on the grid, with CI test, alpha and seed declared in the manifest and its provenance printed on every row. NOT the CPDAG of the true DAG. (2) X is drawn from nodes lying in or adjacent to a chain component of C-hat_E of size >= 2; Y is drawn from possible_descendants(X, C-hat_E) minus {X} — from C-hat_E, never from G0, because G0 = Meek(C-hat, K) is knowledge-dependent, so a G0-based frame hands every knowledge arm a different candidate set, unpairs the instances, and gives the vacuous arm the largest frame and the smallest yield. (3) THE FIXED DENOMINATOR. The frame is frozen once and every K-dependent condition becomes a status: ok / knowledge_inconsistent / k_refers_to_non_edge / k_partially_applicable / o_g0_not_identified / o_g0_empty / z_invalid_at_g0 / o_g0_extensions_intractable / budget_censored. Every arm reports the same denominator with a different status composition, printed as a column. (4) DELETED from the deployment path, all five true-DAG reads in benchmarks/measure.py#fast_gate: `y not in dag.descendants(x)`; `optimal_adjustment_set_dag(dag, x, y)`; `is_valid_adjustment_set_dag(dag, x, y, frozenset())`; the no_valid_adjustment_set branch; and the `knowledge_to_recover(dag, cpdag)` loop behind no_atomic_perturbation_changes_validity. Each becomes a post-hoc audit LABEL attached to a retained instance. (5) DISTINCT-PAIR ARITHMETIC, because the census in circulation is inflated three-fold. Verified: instances.jsonl holds 115,974 non-meta rows over 38,658 distinct (network, x, y) pairs, each screened at coverage 1.0, 0.5 and 0.25, and the coverage-independent rejections are each exactly three times a distinct count (22,110 / 14,206 / 1,246 / 553). Every rejection census is divided by the number of coverage levels before it is quoted as a pair count. The screening universe is itself a per-network subsample of the ordered-pair space at wildly unequal inclusion rates, so `inclusion_probability` is recorded per network and every pair-weighted aggregate is either inverse-probability weighted or labelled in its caption as unweighted over a design-unequal sample. (6) NETWORK-WEIGHTED IS THE HEADLINE, pair-weighted printed beside it, zero-yield networks kept in the denominator; the two differ materially (separation >= 2 at 45.4% against 35.6%, r=1 at 61.1% against 71.3%). (7) PER-NETWORK CAP OF 20 by seeded deterministic stride over the sorted frame, never a function of any computed radius, derived from marginal information rather than from an assumed correlation: at the measured rho = 0.398 an instance below cap 12 is worth 0.222 units of n_eff while the thirteenth through twentieth are worth 0.021 each, so cap 20 keeps most of the achievable effective sample for a fraction of the compute and stops barley's 189 instances from setting a pair-weighted headline. Every headline is printed at cap 20 and at cap 50; a headline that moves by more than its interval width is a statement about which networks dominate. (8) THE BRIDGE STRATUM. The 543 legacy admissible pairs are recomputed inside the new pipeline and printed beside their committed twins, so no committed report is orphaned and no reader has to guess whether a change came from the redesign or from the population. (9) RECORD PER PAIR: the valid-adjustment-set CLASS in {0, 1, 2, >= 3, CENSORED} by bounded search with early exit at 2, since the only decision it feeds is whether the incumbent test has power and an exact count is an enumeration this protocol will not pay for; separation with its status STRING; and the network's inclusion probability. (10) THREE SEPARATE PER-INSTANCE TIMINGS (frame and gate, closure, radius search) plus a per-network-run wall on the _meta row; budgets are stated on the wall and never on the row field. (11) The `sampling_rate` field is retired as a headline in favour of the realised integer count, and the dagitty parser's discarded attrs capture group is wired into ParsedNetwork so the author-declared (X,Y) stratum the pre-registration promised finally has rows.

## WHY
The invariant in brief section 1 forbids true-DAG quantities on the deployment computation path, and the ledger's A2 block enumerates the violating filters with counts I re-verified against the file. The frame must read C-hat_E rather than the oracle CPDAG because the frame's own sizing statistics are functionals of the true DAG: summing descriptive_structure.csv over the 39 DAG rows gives 969 nodes inside chain components of 7,062 total, 276 components on 33 networks, and 767 undirected edges of 9,771 — every one of those computed from the CPDAG of the true DAG. A frozen denominator selected by the truth is the same defect as select_knowledge, one level up. Henckel, Perkovic and Maathuis (arXiv:1907.02435, JRSS-B 84(2):579-599) establish that O* = pa(cn(X,Y)) minus forb(X,Y) is a LOCAL functional, so the query policy determines the answer rather than merely sampling it, which is why the frame must be declared rather than emergent; Schubert, Claassen and Magliacane (LOAD, arXiv:2510.14582) license a strictly local policy by recovering O* around a target pair without learning the global graph. Su and Henckel (arXiv:2206.07533, verified CONFIRMED) make the valid-set count load-bearing, since their over-identification test needs at least two valid sets to have any power. The fixed denominator is the ledger's non-comparable-strata rule turned into a mechanism: admissible instances collapse 543 to 182 to 106 across coverage while survivors gain separation, so the falling r=1 share of 69.8 / 51.1 / 34.0 is composition and not knowledge. The budget currently sits on the wrong side of the filter — 115,974 screens for 831 admissible on 25 of 39 networks, with pathfinder spending its entire 1,200 s wall cap on screening and producing no radius across three censored runs.

## REJECTED_ALTERNATIVES
Screening all n(n-1) ordered pairs and rejecting post hoc: rejected on the measured budget failure above. A frame drawn from G0: rejected because it unpairs the arms, which four of the six candidate designs asserted was impossible while writing a frame that could not deliver it. A frame drawn from the ORACLE CPDAG: rejected because its own size statistics are true-DAG functionals, which is the leak an invariant enforced on quantities rather than on decisions cannot see. Re-deriving the frame per statistical panel: rejected twice — it unpairs P-ORACLE from P-EST and restores the composition confound the fixed denominator exists to kill, and it multiplies the elicitation budget by the whole Pi-by-seed grid, which no stated budget carries. Pair-weighted headlines: rejected on the measured concentration. An exact valid-adjustment-set count: rejected because the enumeration is precisely the cost A5 removes and a bounded class of {0,1,2,>=3} is all the comparison needs. Keeping `sampling_rate` as a headline: rejected because it records the target fraction while `sampled_pairs` records the stride realisation, and the two disagree by up to 23% (hailfinder: rate 0.812, stride 1, 3,080 of 3,080).

## FALSIFIABLE_CHECK
THE FILTER-REMOVAL CHECK, restated as a PAIRED within-instance contrast because the marginal form is not executable. On the 543 bridge pairs plus the distinct pairs the deleted filters removed (553 under no_atomic_perturbation_changes_validity, 1,246 under empty_set_trivially_valid), compute the per-instance change in status and radius and cluster-bootstrap the PAIRED difference over networks; the marginal shares are printed as descriptives only. The marginal version is dead on arrival and this is why: the committed r=1 share is 61.1% with a network cluster-bootstrap 95% interval of [43.8%, 78.8%], a width of 35.0 points, so an 11.1-point threshold sits inside the interval and can never be reached, and the interval already contains the 50% at which the sentence it was built to invert would invert. PRE-REGISTERED DIRECTION: the r=1 share FALLS and the UNREACHED status rises above the currently reported 0 of 831, because no_atomic_perturbation_changes_validity certifies that some single retraction of the full true-derived recovering set already breaks validity, which is exactly what manufactured that zero; a real-side UNREACHED rate that stays at zero once the gate is removed means the filter was never load-bearing and that sentence is withdrawn. SECOND RULE, guarding the frame against selecting for the answer: the frame's undefined-separation rate is PREDICTED TO RISE, because returning the 1,246 distinct empty-set-trivial pairs returns instances whose O(G0) is empty or near-empty and whose separation is therefore undefined. A rate that FALLS below the committed 44.3% means the frame selects for pairs where the radius is trivially defined; the frame is then re-specified and the population-shaping report leads the results section instead of sitting in an appendix. THIRD RULE, the admissibility gate that decides whether the protocol can run at all, with its threshold DERIVED rather than round: over the 2,342 distinct candidates the observables-only filters admit, the committed ok shares are 46.8% at coverage 1.0, 31.4% at 0.5 and 28.1% at 0.25. The partial-coverage cells are the closest available analogue of elicited incomplete knowledge, and both fall below 40%, so the pilot gate is pre-registered at 28.1% with its derivation printed. Below it, the elicited-knowledge deployment arm has no population, the budget is not spent, and the protocol falls back to reporting r on the canonical GAC-valid set alone with the O-based second radius reported on its own sub-population. FOURTH RULE: o_g0_empty is pre-registered as a HEADLINE outcome with a prior of roughly 53% (1,246 of 2,342 distinct candidates, coverage-independent), because it is the honest denominator of every applicability claim the paper will make, and the observable predicate that will produce it is not the true-DAG predicate that produced the 1,246, so that figure is a prior and not a prediction. FIFTH RULE: the measured frame size must be at least 2,342 distinct candidates per coverage, because the observable possible-descendant test is strictly WEAKER than the true-descendant test it replaces; a smaller frame is a bug in the replacement and halts the run.

## COST
The frame itself is graph-only — component membership plus one possibly-directed reachability pass — and runs over the 38,658 already-enumerated distinct pairs in under a minute, so the frame change is not a cost item. What costs is the downstream evaluation of a larger population, and that is priced in A5 and is not knowable until the pilot measures the distribution of undirected edges in G0 under elicited K: every timing in the tree was taken at g0_undirected_edges = 0, so the committed 7.16 s total and 0.16 s slowest instance say nothing about what a partial K costs. Agent-hours about 12: the frame and its before-and-after population report, the status vocabulary and the fixed-denominator machinery, the bounded valid-set classifier, the ParsedNetwork attrs field, the inclusion-probability and realised-count fields, and the three-way timing schema. Human-hours about 2 to sign the frame before it runs, since it is the choice that determines the answer.

## OPEN
The true frame size, which is a LOWER bound of 2,342 distinct candidates per coverage rather than a number, and which must be measured before any budget is set. Whether C-hat_E's chain components are stable enough across the Pi grid for one reference discovery run to serve the whole panel, measured as the adjusted Rand index of the partitions across seeds. Whether the estimated frame's overlap with the oracle frame is high enough that any P-ORACLE row may be cited beside a P-EST row at all, or whether the two panels describe different populations.

# A3 — KNOWLEDGE MODEL — where K comes from: source, coverage, granularity, and above all its error process

## DECISION
benchmarks/measure.py#select_knowledge is DELETED from the deployment path together with its call to demo/example.py#knowledge_to_recover; the same oracle call is also removed from fast_gate, because removing it from one place is not enough. K comes from ONE frozen, hashed, observables-scoped questionnaire answered by four independent suppliers, plus two deployment controls and three audit-arm generators. THE QUESTIONNAIRE: one question per undirected edge of the frozen C-hat_E lying in a chain component that contains at least one candidate treatment under the A2 frame — derived from C-hat_E and never from the set a true-DAG gate happened to admit, because a questionnaire scoped that way is itself a true-DAG functional on the deployment path. Elicitation unit is the CHAIN COMPONENT queried for causal ORDER, plus triplet queries, never pairwise. Node presentation order inside each component query is randomised under a recorded seed. Response grammar {A->B, B->A, DECLINE} across four granularities (required edge, forbidden edge, tier, ancestral), each carrying a retained source-reported confidence. Networks earn questionnaire space by a written yield rule (at least one candidate pair per question asked), and the cut is published rather than silent. A COMPELLED-EDGE CONTROL BLOCK is included: a seeded sample of edges C-hat_E already orients, where a correct answer is redundant under Meek and a wrong one is inconsistent, making it a pure commission detector that costs the deployment path nothing. FOUR SUPPLIERS, all deployment-arm. D-LLM: surrogate, temperature 0, one pass, refinement budget declared and reported as 0, mandatory cache whose key is (model id, hash of the RENDERED prompt after permutation, naming condition, replicate seed) with a startup assertion that two replicates of the same component produce two distinct keys and a printed per-arm cache-hit count, model id and prompt hash in the manifest, K frozen and hashed before any radius is computed. D-LLM-PRIME: the replicate, DECOMPOSED rather than pooled into delta_instrument (same model, two order seeds) and delta_source (two model families) as separate columns, with the design fixed in advance and never switched post hoc on the value of the statistic being calibrated. D-PUB: harvested from the eight author-named source publications, LLM reader first and human adjudicator second, every assertion carrying a page-anchored verbatim quote and dropped if the quote is not verbatim in the source window, restricted to claims within graph distance 2 of {X} union O(G0) with a written widening trigger, and DECLARED UNDERPOWERED for any two-sample test before it runs. Stage 1 of the harvest is exactly six networks ordered by admissible yield, because the paired sign-flip permutation floor is 2/2^k, so k=5 cannot reach p < 0.05 at all and k=6 reaches 0.031. D-HUM: a recruited analyst round on the domain-legible subset, one sitting, no feedback, no revision, capped at 100 minutes, unanswered items recorded NOT-REACHED as a status distinct from DECLINE, size derived from the Wilson curve (recruit to five, floor three), publishing its median seconds per question as a first measurement since the ledger records that no literature on elicitation cost per edge exists anywhere. TWO DEPLOYMENT CONTROLS. D-RAND is the vacuity control and it is NOT K empty: verified from search/exact.py#retractable_edges, which keeps only edges directed in g and undirected in the CPDAG, an empty K gives an empty retractable set, local_up_covers returns nothing, and the radius is UNREACHED on 100% of instances by construction — a column of sentinels the sentinel discipline forbids averaging. D-RAND is size- and placement-matched: same |K|, same granularity mix, same distance-from-X histogram as the D-LLM draw on that instance, orientations drawn uniformly among Meek-consistent choices, redrawn on FAIL under a declared retry cap with cap-exceeded recorded as its own status. D-DEGEN keeps K empty as a DEGENERATE REFERENCE reported as a status line with its identification composition, never as a radius row. THREE AUDIT-ARM GENERATORS, below the rule and flagged true_dag_on_path = via_K: A-TRUE (consistent AND true but incomplete, matched per instance to D-LLM on |K| and the distance-from-X histogram), A-SYN (A-TRUE corrupted by the tiered misfiling operator already sitting deterministic and unrun in synth/knowledge.py, with realised post-filter wrongness reported under a DECLARED estimator and both printed — on wrong_knowledge.jsonl the ratio-of-sums is 6.81% against a per-instance mean of 3.99% for an intended 10%, a 71% relative gap — and with the instance set held fixed across rates so the cells are comparable populations), and A-ORACLE (knowledge_to_recover retained solely to reproduce the committed numbers and to score an elicited K). WHAT EVERY K EMITS, closing the ledger's fourteen checkables with the five the drafts dropped: the reported error object is the CONSISTENT-BUT-FALSE fraction and is never called noise; error is reported on one scale AND restated on the same scale as CSCD's 0.3 reversal rate and b-LOAD's rho and mu; commission and omission are separate axes and w counts only FALSE ASSERTED CLAIMS, because retraction acts only on things that were asserted, so an omission cannot be repaired by the operator and moves the radius only through |K_G0| and through where G0 sits, with omission reported separately as coverage over orientable edges; error is reported by distance from X AND by EDGE TYPE (data-compelled against orientable); the graph-space clustering permutation test is run BESIDE a text-space test against prompt position, which the randomised presentation order makes identifiable; and confidence is retained but QUARANTINED, unreadable by the deployment arm until the audit publishes the calibration curve of stated confidence against measured correctness. Plus pre-filter K, post-filter K and the Meek rejection rate; coverage per granularity, never pooled; cascade size; cycles emitted before repair; stratification by assertion type AND by network; and taxonomy.classify() implemented over the {ORIENTATION, ANCESTRAL, TIER, SPURIOUS, MISSING} x {BENIGN, EFFICIENCY, VALIDITY, UNIDENTIFIABLE} grid.

## WHY
This is the largest defect against the invariant, and the repository is worse than the ledger states: select_knowledge calls knowledge_to_recover(dag, cpdag), and I verified that g0_undirected_edges = 0 in ALL 831 admissible instances at every coverage level, not merely in the 543 at coverage 1.0 — the ball is centred on a fully oriented graph in the entire committed corpus. knowledge_to_recover is also greedy and cascade-maximising, biasing |K_G0|, the other binding term of the bound, downward; an expert states what they know, not what propagates furthest. Triplet and causal-order elicitation is forced by Vashishtha et al. (arXiv:2310.15117, verified CONFIRMED): pairwise edge queries to an imperfect expert produced over 3,000 cycles on Child and over 5,000 on Neuropathic where triplet queries produced none, with SHD falling 177 to 28, and Child, Asia, Alarm, Insurance and Sachs all sit in our own BIF corpus, so pairwise elicitation would make part of the measured error an artefact of our own instrument. Per-granularity coverage is required because tiers close under Meek R1 alone while single-edge assertions cascade through R1 to R4, so equal nominal coverage buys a different closure and therefore a different radius (Bang and Didelez, citation conflict recorded and unresolved). All four granularities close the weakness all four b-LOAD reviewers and the metareview named, since that paper's noise study is confined to required edges. Confidence quarantine follows our own CSCD measurement that soft beats hard under noisy knowledge (PC recall 0.577 to 0.526 under noisy hard constraints, restored by a lambda penalty) together with the ledger's correction that stated confidence is data about the expert rather than evidence about the edge. The vacuity control is forced by Gervet et al. (JEDM 2020), where an expert-designed structure added at most +0.01 AUC on seven of nine datasets — but that is a comparison against a NON-VACUOUS alternative, which K empty is not. The harvest is restructured so the LLM reader initiates and the human adjudicates because the vault's own measurement says tasks with a waiting counterparty close at 54% and self-initiated tasks at 0% over 40 observations. AutoElicit (arXiv:2411.17284) fixes the reporting format for a surrogate and nothing about its accuracy; arXiv:2606.12071 is why no second model may ever validate elicited knowledge.

## REJECTED_ALTERNATIVES
Tuning select_knowledge to a lower coverage: rejected because the K is still read off the answer and because the coverage strata are non-comparable populations. K empty as the null arm: rejected on verified code — it produces UNREACHED sentinels on every instance, and a table column of sentinels would be read as 'no failure found anywhere', the opposite of what a null is for. Pairwise edge elicitation: rejected on the measured cycle collapse. A single scalar error rate: rejected because commission and omission are structurally independent axes. Folding omission into the audited w: rejected because the operator cannot retract an assertion that was never made, so a headline built that way measures a repair the geometry cannot perform. Thresholding confidence to a binary: rejected because the scaffold retains it by design and because thresholding forecloses the soft-constraint comparison. A second LLM as validator or pairwise LLM comparison: rejected because judges and domain experts reach opposite conclusions on the same pairs and the bias is worse in the comparative setting that is the standard remedy. Post-hoc switching of the replicate design when the disagreement rate comes back near zero: rejected because it makes the pre-registered spread criterion satisfiable by which design each network happened to receive. A nine-paper stage-1 harvest: rejected because six is the binding number the permutation floor implies, and nine was never derived. A full-paper harvest: cut to the distance-2 neighbourhood with a widening trigger, and the cut is declared as a prediction rather than an assumption because its supporting pilot was measured on synthetic substrate.

## FALSIFIABLE_CHECK
THREE CHECKS, and the first can retire our own generator. (a) THE CASCADE TEST: compute the distribution of edges compelled per assertion for D-LLM, D-PUB, D-HUM and A-ORACLE on the SAME questionnaire so nothing else varies. PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTION, committed before any answerer is scored: A-ORACLE's distribution is stochastically LARGER, because knowledge_to_recover is greedy and gain-maximising by construction. DECISION RULE: if a paired test on the same edges fails to separate them at the pre-registered level, the claim that the incumbent knowledge model is unrealistically cascading — the stated reason the redesign was necessary — is WITHDRAWN in writing, the incumbent's published numbers stop being labelled biased on that ground, and the redesign's justification narrows to the invariant violation alone. The statistic is cheap and has never been computed on any of the three candidate generators. (b) THE INCONSISTENCY-EXISTS CHECK, the cheapest falsifier of the whole knowledge model: verified, knowledge_inconsistent fires ZERO times across all 115,974 screened rows (it does not appear in the rejection tally, whose six entries sum with 831 to exactly 115,974), so kappa_cons is identically zero in every number this lineage has published and becomes a live variable only under elicitation. PREDICTION, written first: under elicited K the Meek rejection rate is strictly positive on a majority of networks. DECISION RULE: if it is zero or near-zero under real elicitation too, then either the elicitation is too sparse to contradict itself, in which case coverage is reported as the binding limitation and kappa_cons is dropped as a referent, or the surrogate is reciting a consistent structure, which routes to the A6 contamination screen. (c) THE PLACEMENT TEST, which decides the axis: a permutation test on whether the WRONG assertions of an elicited K sit closer together in the graph than a same-count uniform null predicts, run in graph space AND in text space against prompt position, with the test's own validity checked on D-RAND, whose errors are uniform by construction. DECISION RULE: if elicited errors are no more likely to fall within distance s of X than the uniform null predicts, the retraction ball prices a perturbation the real error process does not make and the framework is refuted as an instrument for that knowledge source. If the graph-space and text-space tests fire equally, the finding is about prompts and not about graphs and is reported in those words. If the graph-space test does not reject on D-LLM, the uniform symmetric-flip model that b-LOAD and our own CSCD both used is VINDICATED and the A-SYN correlated-error arm's contribution is withdrawn in writing — a check that can kill a component we want. FOURTH, on the compelled-edge block: it must return a near-zero contradiction rate for D-PUB and D-HUM and a measurable one for D-LLM, or it is measuring question comprehension rather than knowledge and is reported as such.

# A4 — PERTURBATION GEOMETRY — the ball: centre, distance on knowledge sets, operator, and the reference measure inside a shell

## DECISION
Centre at G0 = Meek(C-hat, K) and never at the truth, which the code already satisfies since hybrid.py#breakdown_radius takes no true DAG in its signature and search/exact.py#local_up_covers takes (cpdag, g). TWO UNITS, printed side by side, and the analyst-facing one is the certificate. r_claim, THE CERTIFICATE: the cardinality of a minimum subset S of K whose removal, with Meek re-closure of K minus S, makes Z invalid. Because failure is upward-closed (THEOREMS.md Theorem 1: refinement shrinks the represented DAG set, so orienting one more edge can never invalidate a fixed valid Z), this is the cardinality of a minimum MINIMAL CORRECTION SET, and naming it that buys a solver literature and closes an unrun prior-art search rather than hiding one. Exactness comes from EXHAUSTIVE SUBSET ENUMERATION to a declared depth, which retires the open assumption chain (Anti-Exchange Case B implies Lemma R implies Property S implies Lemma L implies Conjecture 2) whose failure direction is one-sided and optimistic about robustness. r_hop, THE GEOMETRY OBJECT: the BFS hop count on the covering relation restricted to the fixed estimated-skeleton sub-poset, which is what search/exact_fast.py#radius_local_up_fast returns and what compares with the poset literature. They are not approximations of each other and neither is 'the definition'. Verified from source: retractable_edges returns edges directed in g and undirected in the CPDAG, i.e. asserted PLUS Meek-compelled, which is exactly what measure.py writes as k_g0; and local_up_covers keeps only MINIMAL candidates, so a single knowledge retraction can land strictly above a cover and cost more than one hop. Pre-declared direction: r_claim <= r_edge <= r_hop, so the currently reported radius OVERSTATES how many of his own claims an analyst may be wrong about — the same optimistic direction as the open exactness caveat, so two known one-sided errors compound rather than cancel. THE HEADLINE IS SAFE-MOVES = r minus 1, because r >= 1 by construction (Z is read off G0), so the 61.1% of the committed corpus at r = 1 means ZERO safe moves. OPERATOR: retraction, derived rather than assumed, with a flip costing exactly two atomic moves; extended with a TIER BLOCK retraction costing one move in the assertion metric and reported on its own axis, and a FORBIDDEN-EDGE retraction defined for the first time. Ancestral constraints are elicited and NOT perturbed, declared as an open gap rather than faked. BUDGET: exhaustive enumeration to depth 3 with a '>3' sentinel plus a deletion-filtering descent for an upper bound, derived from the committed distribution (811 of 831 instances, 97.6%, have radius <= 3) and from cost (at the corpus median |K_G0| = 10 a depth-3 certification is 176 predicate calls at the measured 1.07 ms, about 0.19 s per instance). THE SHELL MEASURE phi_j IS DEFINED ON THE RETRACTION-SUBSET GEOMETRY and nowhere else: phi_j is the fraction of j-subsets of K whose retraction invalidates Z, computed exhaustively under a declared cap and by 384 uniform draws above it, reported with an O*-relevance-weighted version beside it. The certificate is TWO numbers, r and phi, never one. THE TWO GEOMETRIES ARE UNIFIED IN WRITING BEFORE ANY MEASUREMENT: the covering-graph walk is the definition of r_hop, the subset enumeration is the definition of r_claim and the domain of phi, their j's are not the same metric, and both record method, oracle, assumes and search statistics. NO RANK ARITHMETIC on the MPDAG poset and never SHD as the unit. SENTINEL DISCIPLINE extended in core/conventions.py BEFORE anything new is measured: UNREACHED (validity survives retracting all of K), NO_RETRACTABLE_EDGES (K asserts nothing that could be taken back, distinct from UNREACHED, without which a vacuous arm and a maximally robust one print the same value), CENSORED-EXACT carrying its bracket, CENSORED-BUDGET, NOT-REACHED for elicitation timeouts, and three distinct statuses for r_0's three collapsed infinities. THE DISPATCH LEG IS PRINTED ON EVERY ROW: verified, hybrid.py sets DEFAULT_SEARCH_BUDGET = 3 and dispatches to the ladder on exhaustion, so `method` is a deterministic function of the answer — local_up_fast answered 811 instances with radii strictly in {1,2,3} and e1_ladder answered 20 with radii strictly in {4..14}, switching at exactly the boundary where A1's illustration gate reads. The corpus is re-run at a budget at or above |K_G0| so one estimator answers everything, and where the ladder is still needed a two-leg differential is published with its sign.

## WHY
Taeb, Guo and Henckel (arXiv:2511.10625v3) force the unit discipline: Proposition 12 and Example 2 show SHD1 <= SHD2 <= d_L,pseudo with an explicit instance at d_L,pseudo = 1 while SHD = n minus 1, so an SHD-denominated radius over-certifies; Proposition 9 and Table 3 show the poset is not graded for |V| >= 4, which is why brief section 4 forbids rank arithmetic; section 4.5 and section 7 state three times that the MPDAG covering relation has no known graphical characterisation, which is precisely why a uniform draw from a covering-graph shell is not a procedure anyone can write and the shell must be defined on subsets of K; and section 7 names WEIGHTED COVERING RELATIONS as open future work, the slot the O*-relevance weighting occupies and the slot a follow-up from that group would fill first. The unit mismatch is derived from our own code rather than from preference, and the committed data already show the units disagreeing: the radius exceeds min(s, |K_G0|) in 110 of 463 measurable instances with shapes up to r = 14 against min = 6, concentrated in insurance (55), Polzer_2012 (22) and paths (13). The shell measure closes what the ledger records as simply absent: BFS returns the nearest failure and nothing about how much of shell j fails. The sentinel discipline is not decoration; conventions.py exists because a report and a framing document once disagreed by one, and three consecutive sessions each shipped a bug in the sentinel family.

## REJECTED_ALTERNATIVES
SHD as the unit: rejected on the measured over-certification. A single unit: rejected because the practitioner-facing quantity and the poset-comparable quantity are provably different objects on this corpus. A covering-graph shell measure: rejected as unimplementable, since the covering relation has no graphical characterisation and cannot be sampled from — three of the six candidate designs promised a uniform draw from a covering-graph shell, which is not a thing that can be written. Exactness from the Anti-Exchange chain: rejected in favour of exhaustive enumeration, because the chain's failure direction is favourable to the hypothesis under test and a certificate resting on a conjecture is not yet a certificate. The 0.43 to 0.47 conservativeness as a pre-registered expectation for phi: rejected because it was measured over the whole outside-radius region of a 48-element six-node demo and report.md's own limitations section disclaims it verbatim; it is carried as motivation with its scope printed, not as a prior. A 0.05 informativeness threshold for phi: rejected because phi's own resolution at r = 1 is 1/|K_G0|, median 0.10 on this corpus, and the 400-draw Monte Carlo standard error is 0.025, so the threshold sits below the smallest step the statistic can take. A default depth beyond 3: rejected because 97.6% of the committed corpus is inside it and the marginal budget buys nothing.

# A5 — STATISTICAL LAYER — the oracle CPDAG against a CPDAG estimated at finite n, sample sizes, estimator, and how the two are kept separate

## DECISION
LEVER 0 FIRST, BLOCKING, AND IT IS AN ADMISSION CRITERION RATHER THAN AN OPTIMISATION. Replace demo/evaluate.py#optimal_adjustment_set_mpdag — which computes the DAG-level optimal set in EVERY extension of G0 and returns None the moment they disagree — with the closed form O*(G) = pa_G(cn(X,Y)) minus forb(X,Y), assembled from the polynomial pieces already in the tree: mpdag_criterion.criterion.causal_nodes, forbidden_set, is_amenable, mpdag_criterion.paths.possible_descendants, and gac.mpdag_level.gac_causal_children and gac_forbidden_set. I verified why this is blocking: g0_undirected_edges = 0 in ALL 831 admissible instances, and o_g0_not_identified fires 286 times at coverage 0.25, 232 at 0.5 and ZERO at coverage 1.0. The mechanism is mechanical — incomplete knowledge leaves undirected edges in G0, extensions then disagree about O*, and the instance is deleted — so the pipeline systematically removes exactly the instances where the analyst's knowledge is incomplete, which is every real deployment. There is not one partially-oriented analyst graph anywhere in the measured corpus, and a realistic sparse elicited K therefore yields an EMPTY admissible population on current code. Lever 0's first output is one number reported before any radius: how many admissible instances retain an undirected edge in G0. THE FALLBACK IS WHAT MAKES LEVER 0 SAFE: the certified Z is the CANONICAL GAC-VALID adjustment set on G0, which exists whenever any valid set does, with r_opt against O(G0) reported as a SECOND radius on the sub-population where O(G0) is identified — a construction that survives even if the closed form proves merely VALID rather than OPTIMAL on MPDAGs, in which case the target is renamed and every r_opt claim drops. The predicate is also SPLIT THREE WAYS — identified-nonempty / identified-empty / not-identified — because measure.py's `if not o` conflates None with the empty set, so the committed 518 cannot be decomposed into the two statuses the protocol declares. THE VALIDITY GATE IS NEVER THE ENUMERATING PREDICATE. is_valid_adjustment_set_mpdag leaves production entirely and survives only inside the differential. In production each candidate is decided by a pair of SOUND one-sided tests: a constructed failure witness (one consistent DAG extension by Dor-Tarsi, tested by the back-door criterion, which PROVES failure) and gac.mpdag_level.is_gac_valid_mpdag, whose positive verdict CERTIFIES validity, with CENSORED as an explicit third outcome carrying a bracket rather than a guess. Making the substitution sound rather than merely fast matters because an implementation that over-declares invalidity under-estimates r, which is the safe direction. Wherever enumeration genuinely remains it is restricted to the DEPENDENCY-CLOSED COMPONENT SET — the union of chain components meeting cn(X,Y) together with their parents and neighbours — and NOT to X's chain component alone, because O* reads parents of the whole causal-node set and those can lie in other components; shipping the narrower claim would make the differential fire and kill a substitution that is correct at the right scope. MAX_G0_UNDIRECTED_FOR_EXTENSIONS is set from a measured, STRATIFIED cost curve rather than from a round number, because extension counts are not monotone in edge count. THE COST FACTS ARE RE-MEASURED, NOT QUOTED. The 1,839.78 s figure has no committed artefact in the repository; it is an n = 1 unreplicated stdout capture from a different experiment in the vault, and the call it timed passes the CPDAG with K empty rather than G0. It is replaced by a cost DISTRIBUTION: sweep the number of undirected edges in G0 over a declared grid, at least five repeats per cell, publish the curve with its dispersion and the cell where it crosses the wall budget, and cite every artefact by absolute path with a hash. Likewise the per-instance `seconds` field is not wall time — pathfinder's 1,416 rows sum to 0.171 s of recorded time against three runs each censored at wall_until_timeout_s = 1200.0 — so every budget is stated on the wall. TWO ARMS, SEPARATED BY A BYTE COMPARISON RATHER THAN A LABEL: every record carries chat_source_sha256 and generating_dag_sha256, and where they are EQUAL the record is in the audit arm by construction whatever it is labelled. P-EST estimates C-hat at finite n with a CPDAG-EMITTING method (PC with declared CI test and alpha, plus GES), never a front end that emits a fully oriented DAG, because a skeleton-times-order factorisation commits to one topological order and destroys the undirected chain component the radius is defined on. P-ORACLE is an IDEALISATION CONTROL ARM carrying true_dag_on_path = via_Chat_only; only P-EST may carry a deployment claim, and the claim form 'robust under an estimated CPDAG' stays forbidden until it runs. Both plot on Pi = n / (|V| * d_exp/2). THE ESTIMATOR is the Frisch-Waugh-Lovell standard error from Sigma-hat alone, the same formula serving population Sigma and sample Sigma-hat, with the true DAG entering only to generate the sample by ancestral simulation. THE 95% BAND, NEVER THE POINT HULL, on a measurement rather than a preference: the point hull errs DANGEROUS 24.7% at n = 200 and still 18.1% at n = 20,000 while the band errs at most 1.7% at every n, even though the band's raw agreement looks worse (7.8% against 57.0% on magic-irri at n = 200); a danger rate barely improving across a hundredfold sample increase is the argument that this is a coverage problem and not a sample-size problem, so the budget buys replicates rather than larger n. Coverage is reported CONDITIONAL ON r, never marginally. Say once and early which of three the certificate is: a DETERMINISTIC COMBINATORIAL functional of (C-hat, K, X, Y, Sigma-hat, n), not a Bayesian credible bound and not a conformal set; and keep two reported quantities rather than one interval, because the perturbation layer prices structural error while any conformal layer prices sampling error, and if the graph knowledge is wrong the ESTIMAND itself moves, which is outside any covariate-shift correction. SCOPE CUT, stated rather than discovered: the finite-n arm runs on the networks eligible by a written rule (at most 60 nodes with a chain component of size at least 3), at three sample sizes with at least ten seed replicates, under a per-run wall cap with censoring recorded, so NO finite-n statement may be made over the corpus and the table says so. The fixed denominator cannot be fixed while the predicate that defines it is being swapped. (d) THE ESTIMATED-CPDAG STRUCTURE TEST, TWO-SIDED, because both directions are live and only one was ever anticipated: PC's missed weak edges destroy v-structures and can INCREASE the undirected fraction rather than shrink it. DECISION RULE, DOWN: if at the largest Pi the median undirected fraction falls below half the oracle's 7.85% assertable share (767 of 9,771 edges, verified), the headline changes from 'the breakdown radius on real structure' to 'the radius is defined on a vanishing fraction of a discovered graph', and that becomes the paper's finding rather than a limitation — plausible, since 6 of 39 networks are already fully compelled and only 25 of 39 yield any admissible instance under the oracle. DECISION RULE, UP: if the undirected fraction rises materially, every oracle-CPDAG number in the project is a LOWER bound computed on the wrong graph, the scope sentence is rewritten in the opposite direction, and the fully compelled and zero-yield networks are re-run rather than excluded, which is why none of them leaves the finite-n denominator. (e) THE THREE-BUCKET CARRY-OVER, with its threshold carried honestly rather than imported. The 1.7% DANGEROUS ceiling was measured on 226 instances over three networks under a pair filter requiring the local knowledge set non-empty, i.e. restricted to components the knowledge orients COMPLETELY — the exact complement of the partial-knowledge population this protocol creates. It is therefore carried as an out-of-scope PRIOR with its selection printed, and re-measured on this protocol's own frame before it gates anything. The rule-of-three arithmetic is printed beside it: an exact one-sided upper bound below 1.7% on zero observed events needs 177 instances per cell, so any cell below that is reported as UNRESOLVED rather than as passing.

## COST
Zero seconds on the enumerating validity predicate, by construction, and that ratio is the single most important cost fact in the protocol. ## OPEN
Whether the closed form is OPTIMAL or merely VALID on MPDAGs, which decides whether r_opt survives as a claim at all; the MPDAG-level GAC forbidden set is verified on about 2.5M cases and not proved, in the direction that could deflate the radius, while the exactness chain is unproved in the direction that could inflate it, so two unproved things sit under this axis pointing opposite ways. The distribution of undirected edges in G0 under elicited K, which is the binding unknown in the whole compute budget. Whether the estimated and oracle skeletons are close enough on any network for the two panels to be cited together, or whether they describe different populations and the oracle panel is an idealisation in its own caption.

# A6 — REALISM AUDIT — the external referent against which the simulated knowledge distribution is judged, and how the certificate's calibration is checked

## DECISION
The referent is BUILT in two layers, and the certificate is scored on a dominance ladder in which the radius must beat the free statistics before anything else is reported. LAYER 1, THE TRUTH-FREE BRIDGE, deployment-legal and running on every tier including the real-data tier where no truth exists. A6-0 MASKED COMPELLED-EDGE PROBE: un-orient an edge the DATA compelled in the ESTIMATED CPDAG C-hat_E — stated as C-hat_E and not C-hat, because masking an edge of the oracle CPDAG and scoring against it reintroduces the oracle on the very path this redesign exists to clear — elicit the surrogate blind on a local window, and score its answer against what the data compelled. No true DAG is read, so it runs unchanged where no truth exists, and it delivers the distance-from-X and edge-type stratification the ledger marks UNSUPPORTED as a by-product. The holdout pool is 9,004 compelled edges against 767 orientable ones, so power is not the constraint; the per-network probe count is min(84, compelled), the 84 derived because a rate near our only anchor (CSCD's 0.3) carries SE <= 0.05 at 0.3*0.7/0.05^2 = 84, with the 26 of 39 networks capped by availability reporting their realised SE. On the tiers where truth exists the same edges are also scored against it and the compelled-versus-assertable CORRECTION FACTOR is measured there and carried. A6-0b STRUCTURE-DEPENDENCE PROBE, replacing the named-versus-scrambled agreement rate: elicit twice on the same real names, once with the chain component as it is and once with the presented component perturbed under a declared seed so that a correct answer must change, and report the fraction of assertions UNCHANGED. A model reasoning from the data-derived structure changes its answer; a model reciting a published graph does not. One unambiguous sign, no truth required, available on every tier. A6-0c SOURCE-OVERLAP PROBE, scored against the PAPER rather than the truth: ask each model cold, from the variable list alone, to name the source publication and reproduce its declared exposure, outcome and adjustment set, with the answer key supplied free by the restored dagitty attrs (verified: 12 exposures, 12 outcomes, adjusted nodes in exactly two files, and one `selected` node in Didelez_2010). Any network where the model returns the paper's own exposure-outcome pair above chance is declared SINGLE-SOURCE, A6-1 is void there and printed as void, and D-PUB is not a second sample on it — because D-PUB is harvested from the same eight papers the surrogate may have read. A6-0d COLD RECALL with a PER-COMPONENT chance level, computed as the fraction of orderings consistent with C-hat restricted to that component, exact where small and sampled where large; a single 1/6 scalar mis-sizes the flag in both directions, since the elicitation unit is a chain component and component sizes in the admissible corpus run from 2 (169 of 831 instances, chance one half) to 85. THREE OBSERVABLE ERROR REFERENTS AND THE CALIBRATION THEY FEED. kappa_cons, the Meek Algorithm-1 rejection rate, computable from the transcript alone. kappa_RoCA, defined only where the valid-set class is at least 2 and STANDARDISED BY DEGREES OF FREEDOM or compared as p-values, because retracting an assertion re-opens part of a chain component and changes how many valid sets are compared, so 'retraction lowers T' would otherwise conflate 'this assertion was wrong' with 'the degrees of freedom fell'; instances where retraction changes the valid-set class are reported in their own stratum. delta, the pairwise disagreement between two independent elicitations, DECOMPOSED and never pooled into delta_instrument (same model, two order seeds), delta_source (two model families) and delta_human (two independent coders on the harvest), with only delta_source entering the certificate and delta_instrument reported as an instrument-stability column. delta_human is also, in its own right, the inter-rater agreement statistic for elicited causal structure that the ledger records as absent from epidemiology and everywhere else. THE BREACH PROBABILITY pi_lo = 1 minus (1 minus kappa)^r replaces the credibility ratio, which is arithmetically dead before it runs: r = 1 in 61.1% of instances with median |K_G0| = 12 at full coverage, so r/(kappa*|K_G0|) falls below 1 whenever kappa exceeds 1/12 and no plausible expert error rate is under 8.3%. pi is denominated in assertions the analyst made, measured LOCALLY over the neighbourhood the radius reaches because O* is a local functional, and resampled from the measured clustering of wrong assertions rather than assumed independent. It is printed as a LOWER BOUND with kappa's interval propagated through it, CONDITIONAL ON r in the cells r=1 / r=2 / r>=3, with the caption stating that at r = 1 it IS kappa — 61.1% of rows, 92.4% at r <= 2 — so the pooled spread would otherwise be produced by between-network variation in elicitation reliability with r pinned at 1. Its low arm is pre-registered as UNINFORMATIVE and never as a surviving certificate, because two elicitations that agree may both be wrong in the same way, which is exactly what a shared pretraining prior produces. The SLACK s = kappa_true/(delta/2) is measured on the audited tiers and its between-network variance tested against the sampling variance implied by each network's probe count; if s is statistically a constant it is reported as one global scalar, and if its interquartile range exceeds a declared factor the truth-free tiers lose the breach column and the paper says why. LAYER 2, THE TRUTH-REVEALED AUDIT, opened only after every deployment row is frozen and hashed. A-CAL-1, THE GUARANTEE TEST, which is what the certificate actually promises and which no existing convention scores: let w_c be the number of ASSERTED claims in K that are false at the truth — commission only — and bucket each instance DANGEROUS if w_c <= r-1 and Z is invalid at the truth, HELD if w_c <= r-1 and Z is valid, SAFE if w_c >= r and Z is invalid, SLACK if w_c >= r and Z is valid. A-CAL-2, the estimation calibration in the existing equal / SAFE / DANGEROUS buckets, P-EST against P-ORACLE on the same instances; the two tests are different objects and are never averaged, and neither is ever reported as an agreement rate, because agreement hides the sign and the sign is the whole guarantee. Plus the full error decomposition (reversed / omitted / invented, per granularity, per network, by distance from X and by edge type, with the consistent-but-false fraction named as the reporting object), the wrong-assertion clustering permutation test, the confidence calibration curve that GATES whether the deployment arm may read confidence at all, and the 5x4 taxonomy, which turns the licensed narrow claim — that the class of orientation errors keeping all valid adjustment sets valid is untestable by consistency checks — into a MEASURED denominator, namely the BENIGN and EFFICIENCY column, rather than an asserted scope limit. THE DOMINANCE LADDER IS THE GATE, NOT A COLUMN: on one fixed instance set and one audited outcome, seven instruments each with a declared decision rule and a swept threshold so that 'operating point' means an ROC and not a phrase — B0 certify-always; B1 component_size(X, C-hat), knowledge-free and known at frame construction; B2 the two-line free rule (predict s where separation is measured, 1 where it is undefined); B3 min(s, |K_G0|); B4 |K_G0|; B5 |K|; B6 r_claim. Every comparison is a PAIRED within-instance contrast against B2 with a network cluster bootstrap, and three verdicts are pre-declared: radius wins, equivalent, or UNDERPOWERED, with the third pre-committed so an interval wider than the margin can never be read as a win. THE FOUR DESIGNED TESTS run as specified with two repairs: A6-1's discriminator is calibrated on a FAMILY of single-coordinate alternatives, one per feature in the pre-registered vector, each perturbing that coordinate alone at a declared effect size, with a per-coordinate minimum detectable effect printed and non-rejections reported coordinate by coordinate rather than as one verdict; and A6-3's positive control runs on the matched instrument arm with its scope limit printed, that only the mechanical arms and the scrambled surrogate can answer on a synthetic substrate, so it tests exchangeability for MECHANICAL knowledge and is silent about the human and harvested arms. DECISION RELEVANCE IS SEQUENCED: stage one is the FIRST-FAILING RETRACTION SET named in domain terms with its severity class and the RoCA verdict beside it — decision-relevant, needing no loss function, and exactly what a count baseline cannot produce, since B4 can return a number and can never return WHICH claim. EVPPI runs only if the harvest completes and the certificate survives the DANGEROUS gate. Every arm ships its generator script, and one machine-written canonical numbers file is declared, with every printed number stamped with artefact and key and a build check that fails when a report contains a numeric literal that does not resolve.

## WHY
The ledger's A6 headline is that no published study measures a domain expert's per-edge error rate, separating omission from commission, on expert-specified causal DAGs scored against a known-true DAG, and that this survived a 1,100-note corpus grep, a seven-dimension web sweep and a gap-fill that exhausted a 200-call budget and was blocked by HTTP 403 at the four journals where the measurement would most plausibly live. So the referent is built. A6-0 is the ledger's corrected Veitch and Zaveri template (arXiv:2003.01747) made concrete: delete an OBSERVED quantity from the baseline data and check whether predicted harm tracks realised harm, explicitly without ground truth, with 'observed' becoming 'compelled by the data'. Cinelli and Hazlett (DOI 10.1111/rssb.12348, verified CONFIRMED) set the bar the brief names, a sensitivity parameter calibrated against an observable quantity, and the breach probability is the form that survives our own data where a ratio does not. Su and Henckel (arXiv:2206.07533, verified CONFIRMED, code released) supply both the incumbent test and the honest scope limit, and are why brief section 4 forbids the global untestability claim. Hu and van der Pas (arXiv:2511.01662v2, verified CONFIRMED) and RoaDS (arXiv:2511.06790) are the nearest competitors for the object itself and are run on our own instances rather than answered in prose. Mourao et al. (arXiv:2603.15928) establish that an author-run coverage audit is worth less than a third-party one, which is why every arm ships its generator script — the missing script for the existing comparator arm is this axis's own counter-example. Sadeghi and AbdAlmageed (arXiv:2603.17405) are why every rate carries two dispersion numbers rather than one. The contamination screen is mandatory because ASIA, ALARM, CHILD, INSURANCE and SACHS all sit in our BIF corpus and in the published evaluation sets of LLM-plus-SCM work, and because CausalSynth (arXiv:2605.17528) documents that LLMs override imposed causal facts with pretraining priors. CSCD's rejection at CDM@UAI 2026 is why the audit must report a decision-relevant quantity: both reviewers read epistemic restraint as a ceiling rather than a strength.

## REJECTED_ALTERNATIVES
The credibility ratio r/(kappa*|K_G0|): rejected as arithmetically vacuous on data already on disk. The named-minus-scrambled AGREEMENT rate as the deployment arm's contamination column: rejected because its sign is inverted, taking the same value when the surrogate recites and when it reasons correctly, and the opposite value when it is vacuous. A single 1/6 chance level for the recitation flag: rejected on the measured component-size distribution. A6-1 calibrated against one alternative: rejected because power is a property of a test against a specific alternative, and A-SYN's tiered misfiling moves the clustering coordinate by construction, so it establishes power there and nowhere else. An unstandardised kappa_RoCA: rejected as a degrees-of-freedom artefact. Pooling delta_instrument with delta_source: rejected because the certificate would then be priced in units of prompt sensitivity, and because an order-invariant prompt would mechanically lower every breach probability with no change whatever to how wrong the knowledge is. Pooling pi across r: rejected because at r=1 it IS kappa. A second LLM as validator: rejected on arXiv:2606.12071. Precision and recall against the Sachs reference network: refused in writing, because that network is inferred from observational plus interventional single-cell data and validated post hoc, so domain-accepted means accepted and not true. The benchmark-consistency rates of 26.7 to 57.9%: rejected as an error rate or a pre-deployment gate, because they are LLM-judged agreement with 38,081 papers. Reporting the necessity headline as a verdict: rejected in favour of a pre-registered UNDERPOWERED stamp, on the measured design effect.

## FALSIFIABLE_CHECK
THE STAGE-0 LADDER, blocking, run on data already on disk before any elicitation budget is released, and it is the check most likely to fire. Recomputed by me from results/axisa3/instances.jsonl: the free rule B2 reproduces the committed radius EXACTLY on 636 of 831 instances (76.5%), on every single instance of 10 of the 25 yielding networks (mediator, Didelez_2010, Acid_1996, child, Sebastiani_2005, ecoli70, hepar2, arth150, munin1, diabetes — 212 instances, zero errors), and to within one on 97.8% overall and 99.4% outside T0, on a certificate whose entire measured range outside T0 is {1,2,3,4}. Even the knowledge-free B1 classifies r = 1 against r >= 2 at 78.0% against a 61.1% majority baseline, and component_size = 2 gives r = 1 in 169 of 169. Meanwhile Spearman(r, |K_G0|) is 0.057 over all 831 (p = 0.102, indistinguishable from zero) against 0.695 for separation on the measurable subset. The set where |r minus B2| >= 2 is 18 instances over three networks — paths 13, Kampen_2014 3, insurance 2 — and A1's illustration gate removes paths, leaving FIVE instances on TWO networks as the entire evidence base for the geometry contributing something a hop count does not. DECISION RULE, with a rescue clause: the radius survives only if the paired AUC difference B6 minus B2 on the audited three-bucket outcome exceeds a pre-registered equivalence margin AND the disagreement sub-population both spans a declared floor of independent networks OUTSIDE T0 and carries the audited outcome differently from the agreement sub-population. The census is reported with and without T0, exactly as the illustration gate reports max radius twice. If the rescue set outside T0 falls under the floor, the paper states that on this substrate the certificate is an exact re-derivation of a hop count and the contribution narrows to phi, the calibration, the named first-failing claim set with its severity class, and the geometry. TWO fallback sentences are pre-registered, one for the count family and one for the geometric family, with the ladder rung that decides which named in advance — because the baseline that will win is not a count, and a pre-registration that commits the wrong fallback has not pre-committed the paper. The ladder is additionally pre-registered WITHIN |K_G0| strata with its direction declared: the free ladder's advantage is predicted to GROW as |K_G0| falls, which is the sparse regime an elicited K produces, so a confirming result cannot be reported as a surprise. SECOND RULE, THE NECESSITY CHECK, powered from the MEASURED intraclass correlation rather than an assumed one: on audited instances where the certified Z is truly invalid, the fraction RoCA does NOT detect licenses the motivating claim only above 50%; between 25% and 50% the positioning changes from 'pricing where testing cannot' to 'pricing as a complement to testing' and the abstract is rewritten before submission; below 25% the gap is closed by an existing published test with released code and the result becomes a negative-results entry whose revive_if names the one-valid-set sub-population. The power arithmetic is printed: at rho = 0.398 and cap 20 the design effect is 8.56, so separating 0.50 from 0.25 at half-width 0.075 needs roughly 1,472 truly-invalid instances, while n_eff is ceilinged at k/rho = 62.8 on the current 25 networks — so the headline is pre-registered as UNDERPOWERED and reported as an interval with its width unless the network count rises, and a pilot measures the truly-invalid share before the budget is committed. THIRD RULE, THE BREACH SPREAD, stated conditional on r and against the free competitor pi(B2) = 1 minus (1 minus kappa)^s on identical instances: report the paired difference and the threshold-crossing disagreement rate at the 0.5 column, and require pi's variance at fixed r to exceed what kappa's between-network variance alone produces at that r. If the radius's exponent does not beat the free statistic's exponent, pi carries nothing the free rule does not and moves to the audit block. FOURTH RULE, THE BRIDGE CHECK, which decides whether the truth-free referent works and can conclude that it does not: extrapolation to the no-truth tier is admissible only if the gap between the surrogate's error on masked compelled edges and its error on assertable edges is smaller than the between-network dispersion of either rate, AND the compelled-edge rate genuinely VARIES across networks rather than being a constant. If either leg fails, the paper states in writing that the oracle-free bridge FAILED and that the surrogate's error rate on a real dataset cannot be estimated by this route; the bias is known a priori — compelled edges are compelled because they sit in v-structures or Meek cascades and are not exchangeable with the edges an analyst must assert — and the check converts a caveat into a measured, reportable quantity. FIFTH RULE, A6-2's SIGN: a source producing systematically LARGER radii than the referent on the fixed instance set is optimistic about robustness, the dangerous direction, and that becomes the headline of the surrogate arm rather than a difference reported in passing. SIXTH RULE, A6-3: if the two-sample machinery does not reject on the matched control, it has no demonstrated power and every A6-1 result is reported as uninterpretable rather than as a negative result.

## COST
The ladder is a join over rows already computed — minutes of compute, zero model calls, and it runs today. A6-0's probes are 1,808 across the corpus, plus the structure-dependence replicate, the cold-recall probes and roughly 72 source-overlap calls; two-sample and permutation tests are seconds once the features exist; the realised-bias leg adds a couple of core-hours. Agent-hours about 30: the ladder with its ROC and decision rules, the four probes, the three-referent plumbing with the degrees-of-freedom standardisation, A-CAL-1 and A-CAL-2, the cluster-bootstrap and design-effect reporting layer, the RoCA port with a written record that its transfer from linear-SEM DAGs to MPDAGs is UNVERIFIED, the 5x4 classify(), and the canonical numbers file with its build check. Human-hours about 18, concentrated on the three things no tool can do: writing the tolerances and the two fallback sentences BEFORE the referent is looked at, adjudicating the harvest audit sample, and the prior-art reads. This axis is where the human hours belong and where they are cheapest relative to what they can kill.

## OPEN
Whether a contamination-clean tier exists at all: the abandonment rule is pre-registered, so if no post-cutoff or newly drawn network is in hand by elicitation start the tier is reported ABSENT and A6-4's decomposition limitation moves into the abstract rather than the appendix — but the hole is declared, not filled. Whether the bridge's correction factor is stable enough to carry to the no-truth tier, which is the weakest joint in the design and the only thing standing between it and an unfalsifiable claim. Whether a loss function for EVPPI is ever written, since the decision-analysis tradition's off-the-shelf answer needs one and the project does not have it.

# BUILD ORDER
- STAGE 0A — THE LADDER AND THE UNIT RECONCILIATION, on the 831 committed rows, zero new compute, under five minutes. It comes first because it is the only step that can retire the framework before any money is spent, and because everything downstream is denominated in the unit it settles: the audit headline counts wrong ASSERTED claims on one side and, as the pipeline stands, a BFS hop count on the other. Ship the dispatch_leg column, the canonical numbers file and the three-way timing schema in the same commit, because every number quoted afterwards depends on knowing which estimator answered, which artefact it came from, and whether its cost was wall time.
- STAGE 0B — THE PRIOR-ART GATE, in parallel because it is human hours rather than machine hours. Eight units, two blocking retrievals, one written verdict each. It precedes the build because if the retraction operator is contraction or a minimal correction set under another name, the contribution narrows and the remaining agent-hours are re-scoped before they are spent rather than after a referee says so. The vault's own record is the argument for the ordering: the competing preprint was named 51 days before anyone read it in full, and the full read then reshaped the differentiation paragraph.
- STAGE 1 — LEVER 0: the closed-form O* and its differential. It precedes the frame because the frame's status vocabulary is defined against the identification predicate, and because the measured population contains no partially-oriented analyst graph at all (g0_undirected_edges = 0 in all 831 admissible instances, with every o_g0_not_identified rejection falling at partial coverage). Until this lands there is nothing for an elicited K to run on, so no later stage has a population. Its output is one blocking number, and the pre- and post-substitution population delta is published as a standalone table before any arm runs.
- STAGE 2 — THE FRAME, frozen on an estimated reference C-hat_E, plus the bridge stratum onto the 543 legacy pairs. It follows Lever 0 because a denominator cannot be fixed while the predicate that defines it is being swapped, and it precedes the questionnaire because the questionnaire's edge set is derived from the frame's chain components. The five true-DAG filters leave the deployment path here and become audit labels, and the before-and-after population table is published in the same commit.
- STAGE 3 — THE QUESTIONNAIRE, derived from the frozen C-hat_E. It cannot precede stage 2: a question set scoped by which pairs a true-DAG gate happened to admit is itself a true-DAG functional on the deployment path, which is the same defect as select_knowledge one level up. This is also where select_knowledge and knowledge_to_recover leave measure.py and fast_gate, and where the sentinel and taxonomy work lands, because both must be declared before any new radius is measured.
- STAGE 4 — ELICITATION AND THE PROBES, gated on the stage-2 admissibility pilot (pre-registered at 28.1%, derived from the committed partial-knowledge analogue) and on a re-run of the stage-0 ladder over the pilot's elicited K. It follows stage 3 because the questionnaire must be frozen and hashed before a single answerer sees it, and it must precede the sweep because K is frozen before any radius is computed. The masked compelled-edge probe, the structure-dependence probe and the source-overlap probe ride the same cache.
- STAGE 5 — THE RADIUS SWEEP AND THE SHELL MEASURE, on the frozen frame with the frozen K. It depends on stages 1, 2 and 4 and on nothing else, and its budget is set from the cost curve measured in stage 1 rather than from any timing taken at zero undirected edges.
- STAGE 6 — THE FINITE-n PANEL, over the eligible networks. It follows stage 4 because K must already be frozen, and stage 1 because the tractability levers decide what is affordable; it is deliberately last among the compute stages because it is the largest single cost and the first thing cut if the stage-0 verdict narrows the paper.
- STAGE 7 — THE AUDIT. It opens only after every deployment row is frozen and hashed, because that ordering is what makes the deployment/post-hoc split a mechanism rather than a promise. A-CAL-1, the error decomposition, the bridge check, the discriminator with its per-coordinate minimum detectable effects, the RoCA co-report, and the ladder re-run on elicited K with the truth revealed.

# SURVIVING OBJECTIONS
- THE DIFFERENTIATING POPULATION MAY BE FIVE INSTANCES. On the committed corpus the free rule reproduces the radius exactly on 636 of 831 and to within one on 97.8%; the set where they differ by two or more is 18 instances on three networks, and A1's own illustration gate removes 13 of them, leaving five instances on two networks. The rescue clause and the two pre-registered fallback sentences make that outcome reportable rather than fatal, but nothing in the protocol can manufacture range that the substrate does not have. If the elicited-K re-run reproduces this shape, the honest paper says the certificate is an exact re-derivation of a hop count on this substrate.
- THE CORPUS CANNOT RESOLVE THE NECESSITY THRESHOLD AND NO AMOUNT OF SAMPLING FIXES IT. At the measured intraclass correlation of 0.398, n_eff is ceilinged at k/rho = 62.8 and already sits at 60.1 on 25 yielding networks, so the corpus is saturated at 96% of everything further pair sampling can buy. Separating a 50% from a 25% RoCA-miss rate at the declared half-width needs of the order of 1,472 truly-invalid instances. The headline is therefore pre-registered as UNDERPOWERED and reported as an interval, and acquiring networks raises the substrate claim's precision without raising the audit's, because elicitation on an acquired corpus is cut.
- THE SUBSTRATE PINCER IS NOT FULLY REPAIRABLE. T0 is canonical teaching material and is excluded from every headline by our own gate; T1's famous members (ASIA, ALARM, CHILD, INSURANCE, SACHS) sit in published LLM evaluation sets; T2 is circular, because the harvested referent is read out of the same eight papers the surrogate may have read, so a non-rejection between them could be shared provenance rather than realism; and T3 has no audit twin by construction. The source-overlap probe voids the discriminator where circularity is detected and the contamination-clean tier carries a written abandonment rule, but if that tier is empty the surrogate's accuracy is reported as uninterpretable and the realism referent rests on six harvested networks and a human round that may not happen.
- THE BRIDGE MEASURES A BIASED SUBPOPULATION BY CONSTRUCTION. Compelled edges are compelled because they sit in v-structures or Meek cascades, so they are not exchangeable with the edges an analyst must assert. The correction factor is measurable where truth exists and the two-legged bridge check can declare failure in writing, but the correction is itself estimated on the benchmark tier and the ledger records that no published structural-matching or transfer criterion for graph FAMILIES exists anywhere. This is the weakest joint in the design.
- THE OBSERVABLE ERROR REFERENT IS A LOWER BOUND WITH UNMEASURED SLACK ON EXACTLY THE TIER THAT NEEDS IT. Two elicitations that disagree prove at least one is wrong, so delta/2 bounds the error from below; two that agree may both be wrong in the same way, which is what a shared pretraining prior produces. The slack is measured on the audited tiers and the low arm of the breach probability is pre-registered as uninformative, but the gap between the bound and the truth is exactly what the audit arm can only measure where a truth exists.
- TWO KNOWN ONE-SIDED ERRORS POINT THE SAME OPTIMISTIC WAY AND ONLY ONE IS RETIRED. Exhaustive subset enumeration removes the Anti-Exchange chain from r_claim, but every previously committed radius still carries it, and the unit mismatch (r_claim <= r_hop) points the same direction: both overstate robustness. The counter-directed caveat — that the MPDAG-level GAC forbidden set is verified on about 2.5M cases and not proved, and could deflate — is carried on every number rather than resolved. A theory-first design would spend the whole budget there and would be right that a certificate resting on a conjecture is not yet a certificate.
- THE ORACLE PANEL WILL STILL HOLD MORE OF THE INTERESTING ROWS. P-ORACLE is flagged via_Chat_only and barred from deployment headlines by a byte comparison rather than a label, which is the honest minimum, but the finite-n arm covers roughly 17 networks against the reference panel's 39, and 24 of the 39 are discrete BIF with no covariance matrix at all, so the parameter-faithful cell is four networks. A referee who reads the flag correctly will notice which panel the interesting rows are in.
- THE HUMAN LEGS ARE THE ARMS MOST LIKELY NEVER TO HAPPEN. The harvest is cut to six networks and restructured so the reader initiates and the human adjudicates; the analyst round has a pre-registered absence fallback. Both are still self-initiated tasks, which this vault measures closing at 0% against 54% when a counterparty is already waiting. If both fail, the realism audit stands on the surrogate and the truth-free bridge alone, with validity unmeasured and only reliability measured.
- THE SOFT-CONSTRAINT GEOMETRY IS DECLARED OUT OF SCOPE, AND OUR OWN EVIDENCE SAYS IT PERFORMS BETTER. CSCD measured soft beating hard under noisy knowledge; the radius is defined on a hard Meek closure; the breakdown radius of a penalty rather than a constraint is not defined anywhere and may not be the same object. Confidence is retained and the omission is written down, which is more honest than the drafts, but it is still a hole where a competing construction already sits.
- THERE IS NO INTERVENTIONAL OR EFFECT-LEVEL LEG. The protocol validates a certificate about adjustment-set validity, not about a realised intervention, and the field is measurably moving toward effect-level validation. The reason is real — no dataset carries both a domain-accepted graph and ground-truth interventional effects — but the answer must be written into the paper rather than left for a reviewer to find.
- THE MATCHED SYNTHETIC ARM SITS AGAINST THE GRAIN OF A FIXED DECISION. Labelling it instrument calibration rather than a substrate tier is defensible and is written once, but a co-author can read it as keeping the declined thing under a new name. Dropping it loses the only positive-control power check the audit has, so this design pays a coherence cost to keep a power guarantee, and the choice belongs to Ze rather than to me.
