\subsection{What the measurements opened after the last design pass}

The design runs ended on 9 September and the measurements landed on 12 and 13 September. Four
candidates below could not have been written before that, and they are the reason this is not
simply a re-reading of the old ranking: the certificate ladder aggregated over every knowledge
condition (T2), the policy table with the refusal rate and the tie (T8), the report table with real
bands and named breaking claims (T3), and the learned-graph scope panel (T7).
