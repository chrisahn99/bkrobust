### The ladder, per arm: over-certification (dangerous), detection, false alarm

The claim certificate's line is split twice. By `truth_status`: `ok` rows are those whose claims all name edges the truth contains and whose outcome is a true descendant; `flagged` rows are the rest, which is a statement about the claims and the query, not about the skeleton, since the outcome can fail to descend from the treatment on the oracle frame too. And by the exact premise of the ledger's structural argument, whether the generating DAG is a consistent extension of the analyst's graph (`truth_extends_g0`): where it is, a false claim is the only way the committed set can be invalid at the truth and the certificate cannot be dangerous; where it is not, the estimated graph itself is wrong in a way no retraction of the claims repairs, so a `dangerous` verdict there measures discovery error, not over-certification of the claims. With no claims at all the bucket reduces to validity at the truth.
