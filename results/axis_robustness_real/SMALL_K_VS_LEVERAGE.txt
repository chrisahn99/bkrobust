TWO DISTINCT FACTS, kept apart deliberately (stratum flip_cov100_bw000)

(a) A median survival AUC of exactly 0 tracks SMALL |K|, not leverage.
    The flip arm's depth grid is fractional, so at |K| = 1 the only available
    corruption is 'reverse everything'. That is a property of the design
    applied to a greedy-minimal generator set, not a property of the radius.

(b) Discordance WITH THE RADIUS additionally needs the radius to vary.
    Among the pinned networks only `paths` has that, spanning r_hop 1..14.
